## Head of Trading & Markets — dedicated .208 Council portfolio

The owner's instruction is a Council head whose job is nothing else other than optimising the auction house, direct trades and market economy. The dedicated `head_trading` family and `office_96` record implement that remit; neither replaces the Treasurer, Quartermaster, bank custody staff, Artisanry or the independent Bank Auditor. It is a reserved High Council appointment associated with native slot 1, not a public application track or an eleventh native rank. No individual is appointed by publication.

The trading head studies observed prices, completed-sales evidence where available, demand, liquidity, item supply and seasonal or event-driven changes. They choose candidate goods and buy/sell timings, compare auction-house and direct-trade net proceeds, negotiate transparent item trades, and manage listings, fulfilment, stock and cash conversion. Every opportunity must include the actual acquisition cost, applicable fees, deposits at risk, expected execution time and downside. An advertised asking price does not prove demand or a completed sale.

Capital must exist and be separately authorised before any guild purchase. The Treasurer controls the approved appropriation, custody and payment process. The trader works within recorded total capital, per-item concentration, stock-age and loss limits, pauses when a limit or market premise fails, and requests approval for a changed mandate rather than borrowing or replenishing funds unilaterally. The independent auditor reviews the records without owning the trading result.

The weekly trading handover reports settled receipts, acquisition costs, auction charges and lost deposits, agreed costs, realised net profit or loss, inventory at cost, aged or unsold stock, sell-through, time to cash, concentration and remaining authorised headroom. Unsold inventory, turnover and unrealised price rises never count toward the million-gold spendable objective. The trader must disclose losses, conflicts and any personal interest in a counterparty or item before a decision; no self-approved related-party trade or concealed personal gain.

This is the trading portfolio's sole operational focus. Donations, dues, member recruitment, appeals, punishments, Workshop commission administration, bank custody, sovereign decisions and independent audit are other officers' work. The role does not permit real-money trade, automation, exploits, harassment, misleading offers or routing around an account restriction. Guild members are never obliged to fund a trading loss, sell below a stated price or buy from the guild. Existing Royal Writs and all held provider gates remain OFF.
