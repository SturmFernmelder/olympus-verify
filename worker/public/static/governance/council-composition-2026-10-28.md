## Justice and the other Council responsibilities

Head of Justice is the alliance Council remit for Justice administration and a fair, unconflicted review route. The Council of Justice remains an independent adjudicative body. Individual hearing-panel members still need case designation and conflict review; panel service does not grant blanket Council membership, an eligible vote, private-case access or enforcement power. Independent audit remains separate from Treasury custody and is not converted into a Council seat by its position on a diagram.

Artisanry, Heraldry, Intake and Systems are current Council responsibilities alongside Treasury, executive coordination, War and Justice. Regional officers, branch emissaries, recruiters, engineering support and local organizers retain their own accepted scopes. A shared department or family label does not transfer an appointment, turn local duties into alliance authority or add a native slot. Vice Guild Master and Chief of Staff are not included in the current named-remit roster merely by implication from Co-Guild Master.
