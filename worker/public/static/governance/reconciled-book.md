# 1 Guild Charter of Olympus

This Charter establishes a voluntary World of Warcraft: Forever community. It is a community governance document, not a contract of employment, a government instrument, or a grant of real-world legal authority. Its proposed offices and procedures do not take effect until ratified. Reading, publishing, or signing a draft does not assign an office, change a game rank, or grant access to any system.

## 1 1 Purpose and membership

Olympus exists to make shared play dependable, welcoming, and enjoyable across its affiliated guilds. Its purposes are cooperation in PvP and raiding, sound stewardship of shared resources, support for crafting and learning, and community events. Membership does not require streaming, donating, raiding, or surrendering an account to another person. Social players, crafters, levelers, and competitive players are members of the same community.

Admission follows the published application and verification process. Initiates receive a named mentor and a clear review date. Advancement reflects the relevant published criteria; friendship, payment, and an officer's private preference are not substitutes. An alt remains linked to its verified owner and does not create a second vote or duplicate entitlement where a rule counts people rather than characters.

Members must observe Blizzard's applicable rules and the community's published conduct rules. For a staff-confirmed Blizzard-mandated character rename, that character must submit a new application, receive a new review, and complete fresh in-game verification. An ordinary rename that preserves the established GUID link does not itself require reapplication. A hold on one character is not an invented finding against every other character of its owner.

## 1 2 Document authority

Applicable platform terms and rules govern all community activity. Beneath them, the Royal Charter and Guild Charter form one constitutional settlement; the remaining order is Guild Statute, bylaws, duly adopted ordinances, then individual appointment instruments and event rules. A lower document may implement a higher one but cannot contradict it. The joint Charters prevail over inconsistent legacy charts, informal instructions, or announcements.

The Royal Charter provides ceremonial expression of that settlement and creates no additional royal or legal powers. The two Charters must be adopted and interpreted consistently; a substantive conflict requires published constitutional correction, not a secret preference for one text. Letters Patent establish only the appointment or affiliation stated in their operative fields. Daily news cannot amend rules or quietly appoint a person.

Every operative instrument must identify its version, approving authority, adoption date, effective date, and any superseded text. Material changes require publication and a reasonable opportunity for comment. Constitutional recommendations require two thirds of the total non-vacant High Council seats, followed by Founder ratification. Urgent temporary orders must state their necessity, scope, expiry, and reviewing authority. No retrospective rule may turn previously permitted conduct into a disciplinary offence.

## 1 3 Founder and final authority

Asmon is the charted Founder and final Olympus-wide authority, subject to this Charter's publication, review, and recusal requirements. The Founder ratifies the common constitution, appoints the High Council, confirms guild affiliation and Guild Masters, and resolves reserved constitutional questions. Final authority carries responsibility to give reasons for consequential decisions and to preserve the distinction between community leadership and private access to records.

The Founder may return a measure for reconsideration or issue a reasoned, recorded decision within the Charters. A case in which the Founder is complainant, material witness, or personally interested receives review by uninvolved adjudicators; the Founder does not act as fact-finder. Any later constitutional intervention must identify its grounds and the independent recommendation. Final authority cannot override platform rules or authorize retaliation against a person using the review process.

## 1 4 High Council and executive coordination

The High Council is selected through accepted appointments rather than earned automatically through activity. It advises the Founder, coordinates common policy, proposes the budget, reviews departmental performance and hears matters reserved by the Statute. High Council is also the second native character rank in the proposed ten-rank ladder. A Council seat, its mandate and eligible vote, a character's native rank and actual system permissions are separate records. A rank label creates no Council seat; this draft and an unissued warrant assign no rank or access. Treasurer is an appointment within High Council, not a separate native rank. The approved draft High Council bank preset includes Withdraw Gold and Modify Bank Tabs for all characters holding that rank; actual application must be separately authorized and verified, with the auditor conflict in section 1.7 resolved.

Council quorum requires eligible participants present to exceed half of the total non-vacant seats, with at least three eligible participants. Recused seats remain in the denominator; recusal never lowers the three-person floor. Ordinary decisions use a majority of eligible votes cast at a quorate meeting. Abstentions are recorded but are not affirmative or negative votes; at least three eligible participants must remain present. A tied motion fails. If fewer than three are eligible, obtain an independent panel recommendation under the Statute; the panel gains no Council votes and cannot bypass quorum. An unconflicted appointing authority selects it. No person may determine the merits of their own case. Vacancies and recusals must be recorded.

Co-Guild Master, Vice Guild Master and Chief of Staff are scoped appointments, not additional native ranks. A warrant must identify whether any duties are explicitly combined or held separately, the delegated and reserved decisions, reporting line, limits and absence arrangements. They may coordinate daily work, agendas, local Guild Master liaison and continuity only within that written remit. Neither a title nor candidate consent replaces the Founder, confers permanent reserved powers or grants command over every guild. The owner's existing website Co-Guild Master appointment is distinct from the governance warrant proposed by this book. That warrant remains prepared, not issued; its scope, dates, issuing and accepting signatures and any further access remain blank or separately governed. Its unissued state does not cancel the existing website appointment.

Each portfolio may have a named deputy. A deputy acts only within a written standing delegation or a recorded temporary substitution. Neither deputyship nor seniority creates automatic succession to the permanent office.

## 1 5 Departments and guild autonomy

Four core portfolios coordinate work across Olympus: Department of War, Federal Treasury, Association of Artisanry, and Department of Heraldry. War may have separate PvP and raiding heads. Ministers set agreed standards, offer support, and coordinate through the corresponding emissaries. They do not command Guild Masters merely because their boxes sit higher on a diagram.

A Guild Master runs the individual guild, appoints its local officers within published limits, manages its roster, and answers to the Founder and High Council for common obligations. Local decisions remain local unless a published Olympus-wide rule reserves them. A disagreement between a minister and a Guild Master goes to the Chief of Staff for coordination and, if unresolved, to the designated constitutional authority.

Each guild should designate emissaries for War in PvP, War in raiding, Finance, Artisanry, and Heraldry. Their departmental reporting is a coordination line; their local authority comes from their Guild Master's appointment. Raid Leader is a native rank in Article S3; actual event leadership is a bounded assignment. Raid assistants, Marshals, PvP captains, quartermasters, recruiters, event coordinators, technical stewards, mentors and Class Heads remain duties or appointments rather than additional native ranks. Recruitment is initially coordinated under Heraldry. Technical support may serve all portfolios without creating a fifth department. Future departments require an actual workload, responsible lead, and ratified amendment.

The proposed native ladder has ten ranks in this order: Guild Master, High Council, Officer, Officer Alt, Raid Leader, Veteran, Raider, Member, Alt and Initiate. Treasurer and Co-Guild Master are appointments, not native ranks. Additional specialist work remains duties, appointments or tags. Native Alt and Officer Alt coexist with verified main/alt identity relationships and create no duplicate person or vote. Class Heads remain AddOn appointments for the original nine classes; their expertise grants no disciplinary or bank authority.

## 1 6 Justice and review

The Council of Justice handles conduct, disputes, appeals, and compliance independently of the department structure. High Councillors without another portfolio form its default membership, but case access and adjudicative authority require designation. A portfolio holder is not entitled to decide a complaint concerning their own department.

A member facing a consequential sanction receives notice, an intelligible account of the relevant evidence, an opportunity to respond, a reasoned outcome, and an appeal route. Private evidence may be summarized to protect another person; the panel must still assess it. Conflicted members recuse. Appeals go to people uninvolved in the original decision. A necessary emergency measure must be limited and reversible, expire after 24 hours unless an unconflicted authorized reviewer approves a written renewal before expiry, and receive independent review within 48 hours. Every renewal states necessity, scope and an expiry no more than 24 hours later; the initiating officer cannot self-renew. Good-faith criticism, complaints and appeals are not emergency grounds by themselves. Restrictions and bans are deliberate authorized decisions, never automatic punishment inferred from an unpaid ledger or a technical flag.

## 1 7 Resources  records  and boundaries

The established dues policy is one gold weekly, a 14-day exemption for new members, and 90-day ledger retention. Officers record actual receipts manually. No bot collects gold, assumes payment, or applies automatic sanctions. Proposed spending requires a purpose and an authorized approver. The Treasurer may not conceal a withdrawal behind an audit entry.

An independent auditor must not hold or retain withdrawal, custody or transaction-approval rights over the assets they audit, including rights held before the audit appointment. Reconcile or remove incompatible access before the audit starts. Audit access is read-only and limited to the relevant logs and records. No person may approve and independently audit the same transaction. Necessary confidential records use role-limited access and published retention; office is not permission to browse them out of curiosity. Privacy requests are monitored each working day by the designated reader, with the established 30-day inactivity retention. Erasure requires ownership checks and the applicable authorized process. Because the approved draft High Council preset grants Withdraw Gold and Modify Bank Tabs to all holders, an auditor retaining those actual rights cannot be described as read-only merely by a different appointment title. An unconflicted operator must establish a compatible real access arrangement before the audit. This draft does not appoint an auditor, demote anyone or change any bank permission.

# 2 Royal Charter of Olympus

**DRAFT FOR RATIFICATION  -  CEREMONIAL INSTRUMENT**

This instrument uses the language of a royal charter as guild fiction and ceremony. Olympus is not a sovereign realm. It creates no real-world nobility, royal status, public office, territorial claim, immunity, or legal privilege. Its operative meaning is confined to the ratified Guild Charter and community rules.

## 2 1 Preamble and foundation

Let it be known among the guilds and companions of Olympus that many guilds shall share one fellowship, and that no banner shall diminish the worth of those who bear another within it. By the assent recorded below, we establish Olympus as a common house of play, service, learning, and mutual regard.

We recognize Asmon as Founder and final authority within the published settlement. We establish the High Council to advise and coordinate, the four core portfolios to serve the guilds, and the Council of Justice to consider disputes with independence and restraint. Each Guild Master shall govern their own guild under the common Charter; each minister shall coordinate through willing and duly appointed emissaries. Service shall be measured by fulfilled duties, not by the grandeur of a title.

## 2 2 Liberties and obligations

Every member shall have the benefit of published rules, consistent application, a hearing before consequential judgment, and a route of appeal. No treasury contribution shall purchase office or immunity. No office shall excuse harassment, deceit, favoritism, or misuse of another person's records. Casual fellowship, craft, mentoring, and competitive achievement shall each have an honored place.

Those entrusted with resources shall keep faithful records. Those entrusted with command shall state its limits. Those entrusted with judgment shall disclose conflicts and recuse. Those entrusted with publicity shall distinguish fact, proposal, and ceremony. A ceremonial seal may authenticate a community record; it shall not substitute for an approving signature, a version, or a lawful platform permission.

## 2 3 Service  succession  and preservation

We permit written appointments for ministers, deputies, emissaries, tactical leaders, mentors, and other necessary servants of the fellowship. Their powers extend only as far as their warrants. On resignation, expiry, removal, or a declared conflict, they shall surrender the delegated duty and assist an orderly handover without destroying records or retaining access by custom.

Let this Royal Charter be read with the Guild Charter as one constitutional settlement, subordinate to applicable platform rules. Ceremonial words confer no wider powers than their shared operative provisions. A substantive inconsistency requires published correction under the constitutional adoption process. Until ratified, this instrument is a proposal and shall be displayed as such.

# Organisation and Offices

The expanded structure uses four core departments, a shared Systems division and five correspondents per affiliated guild. Solid reporting lines identify accountability; coordination lines identify cooperation rather than command. A job can be combined with another compatible job where workload permits, but a custodian cannot independently audit their own assets and a case participant cannot adjudicate their own dispute. The ten native ranks are stated in Article S3 and remain distinct from the appointment and reporting structure.

## Command and constitutional offices

**Founder and Patron.** Asmon is the Founder named in the reference chart. The ratifying authority confirms that identity and the reserved powers in the Charter. The office confirms affiliation and appointments, ratifies constitutional measures and records reasoned final decisions. It does not establish royalty outside the community or remove platform obligations. A conflict concerning the Founder goes to independent adjudicators.

**High Council.** Appointment-selected Olympus-wide leadership advises the Founder, coordinates standards, reviews budgets and monitors performance. High Council is also a native character rank; the accepted seat, eligible vote and mandate are not created by that rank label. Each eligible seat has an accepted warrant and one vote regardless of other offices or linked characters. Portfolio ownership is an appointed Council duty. A draft warrant assigns no rank or system access. Approved native-rank presets and actual verified permissions are recorded separately.

**Co-Guild Master, Vice Guild Master and Chief of Staff.** These are appointments with expressly recorded remits; they are not extra native ranks and are not automatically interchangeable. Any combination of duties must be stated in an accepted warrant. They may coordinate agendas, guild-master liaison, the action register, absence cover and delivery across portfolios without assuming the Founder's reserved powers or command over other Guild Masters. The exact owner-confirmed public display name is richwcampbell., with consent recorded. The existing website Co-Guild Master appointment is distinct from the governance warrant prepared here. **Secretary and Registrar.** This practical duty may be carried by the Chief of Staff or another trusted volunteer. It records instruments, minutes, vacancies and current appointment terms. Its confidential access is limited to maintaining the relevant records. It cannot manufacture approval, issue its own appointment or silently edit a decision after the meeting.

**Council of Justice.** A separate adjudicative body handles conduct, compliance, disputes and appeals. High Councillors with no other portfolio form its default membership, but individual case designation, recusal and access must be recorded. Case panels exclude conflicted participants; appeal reviewers were not involved in the original determination. Actual moderation is executed by separately authorized staff, not by every person with a Justice title.

## Guild management and five correspondents

**Guild Master.** Runs the individual affiliated guild, its roster and local officer delegation, while answering to the Founder and High Council for common obligations. Confirms local practices, capacity, succession and resource custody. Ministers communicate through the Guild Master and appointed emissaries; department position does not confer command over that guild.

**Officer.** Performs local leadership explicitly delegated by the Guild Master. Each sensitive game, Discord, site or financial power needs actual permission. An officer can also hold an emissary or service appointment, but this must be recorded. Holding the native Officer rank does not establish High Council membership or permission to view every other guild's recruitment.

**Officer Alt.** A native rank for a verified alternate character of an officer. The owner/main relationship must be recorded through the applicable verification process. It does not create another person, Council seat or vote, grant command over another guild, or bypass current permission checks. Native Officer and Officer Alt are distinct slots in Article S3.

**Emissary of War for PvP.** One responsible correspondent per participating guild liaises with the PvP head, proposes local participation and coordinates Captains and scouts under the Guild Master's authority. It does not commit that guild to operations without local approval.

**Emissary of War for Raiding.** One correspondent per guild communicates preparation needs and event plans with the raiding head. It coordinates Raid Leaders and assistants but does not replace their event command or the Guild Master's local decisions.

**Emissary of Finance.** One correspondent per guild supplies approved financial summaries and coordinates dues, budget requests and custody with Treasury. It supervises or liaises with the local Quartermaster under the actual delegation. Audit remains independent.

**Emissary of Artisanry.** One correspondent per guild brings crafting and material needs to the Association and coordinates willing suppliers. It does not requisition private resources or infer consent from membership.

**Emissary of Heraldry.** One correspondent per guild closes the missing link in the original chart. It verifies local news, shares approved announcements, coordinates social events and supplies a contact for community publicity. It works with local Events, Recruitment and Media representatives without making every function a separate officer rank.

## Local tactical and support appointments

**Raid Leader, Marshal and Raid Assistant.** Raid Leader is a native rank in Article S3. Event command is a separate bounded assignment by the Guild Master, coordinated through the Raiding Emissary. Marshals and assistants are tactical appointments or aliases only within their stated remit; they are not further native ranks. The assigned leader uses the published roster and loot rules and delegates only named tasks. Actual raid-warning, invitation, bank and other capabilities depend on the client and separately configured permissions. The owner-selected authenticator safeguard is retained, including for the Raid Leader draft preset; this book does not apply it in the game.

**PvP Captain.** Leads a named local PvP activity, briefs objectives and coordinates communications. Reports through the PvP Emissary for shared planning, while local authority comes from the Guild Master. Captains cannot unilaterally bind other guilds or turn performance criticism into a conduct sanction.

**Quartermaster Bank Manager and Bank Steward.** These aliases describe the local stock and custody appointment, coordinated through the Finance Emissary. It issues approved supplies, records stock and receipts, and observes actual limits. It must not serve as the independent auditor of the same assets. Reserve stock for emergencies only under a published local rule.

**Guild Recruiter and Trial Officer.** May be combined in one small guild or divided when workload grows. The Recruiter handles applicants and the Trial Officer coordinates the stated introductory or activity review. Both work with central Intake and the Guild Master, explain next steps and arrange mentorship. Missing data or an applicant's silence is not fabricated proof of misconduct.

**Social Community Officer and Event Representative.** Provides local onboarding socials, inclusive community activities and a liaison to Heraldry. It recognizes non-raiding participation and helps communicate events. It does not grant private staff access or police members' chosen play hours merely by holding the title.

**Stream and Content Representative.** Supplies verified local information or permitted clips to the Media Lead. It obtains the relevant participant consent, observes event recording rules and does not publish private chat logs, cases or financial receipts.

**Mentor and Class Head or Class Lead.** A Mentor supports a named Initiate; a Class Head offers class or specialization advice across guilds. The offices can overlap but are separately identified. Class portfolios in this chart are Warrior, Paladin, Hunter, Rogue, Priest, Mage, Warlock, Shaman and Druid. These remain appointments in the roster of duties; this book does not claim new AddOn badges are already implemented.

## Membership ranks  relationships  tags and review statuses

**Social and Casual.** A voluntary play-preference tag. A full Member retains community standing without compulsory raids or PvP. Access follows the existing verified membership and actual permissions, not an inferior Social rank.

**Alt.** Alt is a native rank in Article S3 and also describes the verified relationship between an alternate character, its owner and a designated main. The native slot does not create another person, vote or presumed duplicate account. It does not authorize automatic roster purges or arbitrary loss of resources. Officer Alt is a separate native slot for an officer's verified alternate. The proposed single-account dues interpretation remains subject to its separate ratification.

**Trial Member Pledge and Trialist.** Aliases for the introductory status normally associated with Initiate. A raid trial is an activity status and preserves an established member's native rank. Every review has criteria, support, owner and date.

**Probation Penal Hold Timeout and Sentry Hold.** A specifically defined case or review status, with scope and review date, imposed only through the relevant authorized process. It does not create an additional punitive native rank or itself change game or Discord permissions. The person may challenge an error and use the appeal route.

**Veteran Officer Honorary and Emeritus.** Recognition for past service or an advisory appointment. It creates no standing access, vote, bank rights or moderation power unless a distinct current warrant grants that specific duty. The native Veteran rank remains a separate membership rank.

## Time commitment and role review

Appointment warrants record an agreed service expectation that reflects the volunteer's availability. A reasonable starting expectation is a weekly check-in for active correspondents, a fortnightly coordination meeting and a monthly portfolio review; these are planning recommendations, not compulsory game hours. Duties can be combined or paused when workload is small. After a six-to-eight-week pilot, review response times, workload, conflicts, permissions and whether a deputy is needed. A vacant office stays vacant and its essential work is assigned explicitly rather than invented in an organisation chart.


# The Guild Statute of Olympus

**Proposed instrument - successor draft R6 (distinct revision of R5), prepared 9 October 2026 (America/Chicago), derived from the preserved 2 October 2026 edition. Authoring date only; adoption and effective dates remain blank.**

This statute and its accompanying bylaws propose rules for the Olympus community and its affiliated guilds. They are not a claim that Asmon, the High Council, guild masters or members have already adopted them. Names shown in the supplied chart describe its source snapshot; an office becomes authoritative only through an accepted, recorded appointment and separately approved permissions. "Royal," "patent," "federal" and similar ceremonial language confer no real-world sovereignty or legal office.

## Article S1   Purpose  scope and controlling rules

Olympus exists for cooperative play, friendship, fair access to guild activities and responsible stewardship of shared resources. Affiliated guilds retain their own leadership while sharing the Olympus constitution, standards and voluntary common services. The initial directory provides Olympus I-X; further guilds may affiliate through an approved instrument identifying their guild master, local rules and responsible contacts. This does not assert how many guilds presently exist.

Applicable Blizzard and Discord terms and platform rules prevail. Subject to them, the Royal and Guild Charters form the constitutional instruments, followed by this Statute, the Bylaws, published ordinances and individual warrants or event rules. A lower instrument cannot amend a higher one. Platform capabilities must be checked before a rule is implemented; no proposed title proves that a game permission, bank facility or API exists.

## Article S2   Admission  identity and membership

An application, a verified character link, guild membership and permission to publish a directory entry are separate matters. Recruitment staff shall explain the admission process, obtain only necessary information and arrange the prescribed in-game verification. An accepted application does not itself grant a game invitation or staff powers. Officers with actual invitation authority complete the appropriate game action.

A member may designate a main character and link verified alts. A verified main/alt relationship may be represented by the native Alt or Officer Alt slot. This classification is not a lesser class of member and does not authorize automatic inactivity purges of a game roster. The inactive-record policy in Article S12a is a distinct data-retention purpose, not a membership sanction. Published attendance expectations apply to a chosen activity, not every social member. Membership transfers require agreement from the receiving guild and reconciliation of the member's home guild; they do not bypass verification.

A staff-confirmed Blizzard-mandated name change requires a new application, fresh review and fresh in-game verification before that affected character is accepted again. Ordinary renames preserving the character's established GUID link retain continuity. No one shall infer a mandated change from spelling alone. Staff assess other linked characters individually rather than automatically punishing the whole account.

Asmon is the charted Founder and final constitutional authority, subject to the adoption and conflict rules in the Charters. ## Article S7   Rights  duties and fair access

Members may obtain the current rules, identify responsible officers, request correction of inaccurate records and raise concerns without retaliation. Activity organizers shall publish eligibility and selection criteria. Non-raiders retain full community membership; role limitations must concern the actual activity or a justified security need.

Members shall respect others, follow platform rules, report serious concerns through appropriate channels and avoid impersonation, harassment, threats, fraud, theft and deliberate sabotage. Constructive disagreement, declining an event and making a good-faith complaint are not misconduct. Welfare contacts may listen and signpost support; they shall not present themselves as medical or crisis professionals.

## Article S8   Justice  restrictions and sanctions

The Council of Justice provides impartial casework, mediation and appeal review. High Councillors without another portfolio form its default membership as the chart provides, but individual case designation, recusal and access remain separate. Neither Council membership nor a default Justice title grants blanket enforcement powers. Only specifically authorized staff may apply an actual platform or game restriction.

Except for a proportionate urgent safety measure, the subject receives the alleged rule breach, a useful summary of evidence, the possible consequence and at least seven calendar days to respond. Sensitive evidence may be redacted to protect others, but a decision cannot rest solely on undisclosed material. Give a reasonable opportunity to identify mistakes, provide context and nominate relevant witnesses. A hearing may be written or held privately with an agreed record.

Decision-makers disclose conflicts and recuse where personally involved, closely connected or a witness to disputed facts. Findings distinguish established facts, uncertainty and opinion. Responses may include advice, a warning, a specific temporary restriction, removal from an appointment or removal from membership, proportionate to seriousness and recurrence. "Penal Hold" records a defined restriction and review date; it is not a punitive native rank or permission grant. An allegation alone is not a finding.

## Article S9   Appeal and reinstatement

A subject may appeal within fourteen calendar days of receiving the written decision for procedural unfairness, material new evidence, an unreasonable finding or disproportionate consequence. A reviewer who did not investigate or decide the original matter acknowledges the appeal within seven days and gives an expected decision date. If independent reviewers are unavailable, appoint an impartial panel rather than recycling the same decision-makers.

The appeal may uphold, vary or return the matter for reconsideration. No automatic increase in sanction follows an appeal. Restore removed privileges through separately authorized operators after reinstatement; do not assume a case record changes actual permissions.

Proposed hardship relief is confidential: the Treasurer and one independent authorized reviewer may defer or waive an obligation with a limited record, without a public list of recipients. Donating more does not purchase rank, votes, preferential case outcomes or loot entitlement.

The custodian records receipts, purpose, recipient and authorization. The auditor independently reconciles balances and exceptions and must not hold or retain withdrawal, custody or transaction-approval rights over the assets examined, including pre-existing rights. Incompatible actual access is resolved before examination; a title alone cannot enforce this separation. Procurement compares reasonable alternatives, discloses related-party interests and confirms receipt. Reversals preserve a traceable correction rather than disguising the original entry. Shared-resource permissions and any game-bank capabilities require separate verification and approval.

Participants can decide whether to join with those rules known. No leader may introduce a personal reservation after the relevant drop or silently change the method during the event.

# Bylaws of Olympus

**Proposed operating procedures subordinate to the constitutional instruments and Guild Statute.** The periods, quorum rules, financial threshold and appointment terms below are recommendations for adoption, not claims about existing software controls.

## Bylaw B1   Appointments and the register

An appointment warrant records the office, guild or portfolio, duties, supervisor, boundaries, start date, review date, accepted appointee and required permissions. Ordinary appointments have a renewable six-month term unless the warrant specifies a shorter pilot. Technical custody may be reviewed more frequently. A vacancy remains vacant until an accepted appointment is recorded; chart labels are not warrants.

The Chief of Staff is accountable for the registers; the delegated Secretary or Registrar maintains the current office register and confidential permission register. An explicitly named acting registrar covers a vacancy without acquiring further authority. A recorded appointment, separately authorized permission change and verified readback are three distinct steps. Removal or expiry requires checking all actual access, service ownership and shared credentials without deleting needed records or disabling recovery.

## Bylaw B2   Council notice and quorum

Ordinary High Council meetings receive seven calendar days' notice with an agenda; urgent meetings receive at least twenty-four hours where practicable. The Secretary records current non-vacant voting seats before the meeting. Vacancies have no vote. Attendance, recusals and declared interests are recorded.

Quorum requires more than half of all current non-vacant voting seats and at least three eligible, unconflicted members. Recusal does not reduce the seat denominator or the protective floor. A councillor with several offices has one vote. Without quorum, members may discuss and recommend but cannot record a binding Council decision. If fewer than three unconflicted members are available, an unconflicted appointing authority commissions an independent panel recommendation. The panel acquires no Council votes and cannot bypass quorum. A conflicted Founder cannot decide the merits of their own case or overwrite the independent finding as its fact-finder.

## Bylaw B3   Motions  votes and constitutional recommendations

Ordinary motions pass by a majority of eligible votes cast at a quorate meeting. Abstentions are recorded but not counted as affirmative or negative votes; there must still be at least three eligible participants. A tie defeats the motion; the chair has no extra casting vote. Written votes use the same membership, notice and quorum rules and a stated closing time.

A proposed constitutional amendment, guild affiliation or permanent creation of a department requires affirmative votes from at least two-thirds of all current non-vacant Council seats, rounded upward, followed by the reserved approval required by the Charters. Publish adopted wording, effective date and reasons. Membership consultation informs these decisions but is not falsely reported as a referendum or unanimous consent.

## Bylaw B4   Minutes and reserved decisions

Minutes identify the proposal, authority, participants, conflicts, vote, reasons, implementation owner and review date. Circulate a draft within seven days and correct errors visibly. Publish the non-sensitive decision summary; protect case and financial evidence. A reserved decision by Asmon must identify whether it affirms, varies or returns a Council recommendation and give reasons. It is entered into the same register rather than conveyed as an unverifiable private instruction.

## Bylaw B5   Emergency measures

A specifically authorized officer may impose the narrowest reversible measure needed to address an immediate credible risk. Record the reason, affected capability and time as soon as practicable, notify the subject safely and seek an unconflicted review. The temporary measure expires after twenty-four hours unless an unconflicted authorized reviewer approves a written renewal before expiry. Each renewal records reasons, precise scope and an expiry no more than twenty-four hours later; the initiating officer cannot self-renew. Good-faith criticism, complaints and appeals alone are never emergency grounds. A reviewer independent of the initiating officer examines necessity within forty-eight hours.

If immediate removal is technically unavoidable, preserve the evidence and an available appeal route. Emergency authority cannot permanently amend the constitution, appoint a council, dispose of substantial resources or erase a complaint. Extensions require an explicit next review date; a rolling hold is not a substitute for a hearing.

## Bylaw B6   Intake  trials and mentoring

Recruitment staff assign a contact and explain which steps are pending. The proposed introductory review occurs within fourteen days of admission. A single further fourteen-day extension requires recorded reasons and support goals; thereafter decide membership or explain why the process cannot be completed. An existing member's raid trial assesses that activity without cancelling unrelated membership.

Mentors help with rules, verification, class resources and first events. Trial criteria concern demonstrated conduct and the chosen role, not undisclosed attendance quotas. Staff record minimal outcomes and the next review date; neither silence nor a missing export counts as proof of failure.

## Bylaw B7   Resource authorization and audit

A proposed substantial transaction is an unbudgeted transfer exceeding ten gold, or any transfer exceeding ten percent of the last reconciled unrestricted balance, whichever threshold is reached first. Apply this procedural threshold to combined related transfers over seven days to prevent splitting. Where no reliable balance exists, treat every non-routine transfer as substantial until reconciliation.

Substantial transactions require two recorded approvals: the responsible Treasurer or guild master and an independent, unconflicted authorized approver. Neither recipient may approve their own payment. Record authorization before the separately permitted custodian executes it. Publish routine budget ceilings; perform weekly reconciliation and monthly independent review. Suspected loss preserves logs and triggers investigation, not a public accusation. The auditor remains independent of withdrawal custody.

## Bylaw B8   Complaints and case administration

A complainant may approach a designated local officer, welfare contact or Justice case officer. Acknowledge a received complaint within three working days, identify an unconflicted case owner and explain likely timing. Attempt mediation only when suitable and voluntary; serious safety concerns need not pass through mediation first. Limit access to those handling the case.

Case records state allegation, applicable rule, evidence sources, response, finding, consequence, implementation authority and appeal deadline. Separate administrative membership verification from a misconduct finding. A moderator enforces only the recorded measure within actual permissions; broader enforcement requires a new decision or justified emergency measure.

## Bylaw B9   Operational cadence and continuity

Each active guild reviews intake and urgent member needs weekly. Portfolio heads and emissaries coordinate fortnightly; the Council reviews budgets, vacancies, risks and service performance monthly. These are proposed cadences, not compulsory play hours. An unavailable officeholder designates a permitted deputy or asks for cover; essential custody has a documented recovery contact.

Technical releases need an identified owner, reviewed changes, recovery arrangements and current evidence of actual deployment. Daily news may report confirmed changes, forthcoming events and approved appointment notices. It shall label proposals and corrections, exclude confidential allegations and avoid claiming that a drafted rule is live.

## Bylaw B10   Adoption and implementation

First adoption uses the inaugural route in Guild Charter 1.8: confirmed Founder assent after at least seven calendar days of consultation, at least three named independent reviewers with recorded recommendations, and each participating Guild Master's assent for their own guild. This does not invent a Council quorum or appoint the reviewers. For later ratification or amendment, use the ordinary constituted Council and reserved approval rules. Confirm authority, resolve objections and approve exact versioned wording. Record the applicable inaugural recommendations or actual constituted Council recommendation and constitutional assent with actual names and dates; leave signatures blank until obtained. Publish the effective instruments and a short member guide.

Then issue appointment warrants, review permissions individually, verify the implemented access and pilot new duties in one guild for six to eight weeks before wider adoption. No appointment is filled merely because this draft suggests it. Ratification authorizes policy; operational changes still require competent operators and the relevant approvals. Review the adopted package after the pilot and annually thereafter without rewriting the historical decision record.
