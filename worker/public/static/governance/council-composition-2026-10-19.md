# High Council composition — owner direction recorded 19 October 2026

Status: owner-directed composition successor; constitutional process pending. This is not an adopted R8 charter or a record of a Council vote.

The project owner explicitly directed that the Co-Guild Master should also be part of the High Council. The current organization view therefore includes the Co-Guild Master alongside the five core portfolio offices: War Head for PvP, War Head for Raiding, Federal Treasurer, Minister of Artisanry and Minister of Heraldry. These offices are not a six-seat limit; other accepted councillors and recorded portfolios remain possible.

The adopted R7 Guild Charter, section 1.2, requires a constitutional recommendation supported by two thirds of the total non-vacant High Council seats, followed by Founder ratification. No such recommendation, vote or ratification is recorded by this publication. The original R7 adoption remains 13 October 2026. Its current book, operational charter and historical downloads are preserved; this dated composition direction is published separately rather than silently rewriting them.

The direction concerns the Co-Guild Master office. It does not add the Vice Guild Master or Chief of Staff by implication, issue an individual governance warrant, fill a vacancy, grant an eligible Council vote or apply a native rank. Individual acceptance, mandate, dates, issuing authority and signatures remain separately recorded. An existing website appointment is not revoked by the pending constitutional process, nor does the website appointment alone complete it.

One person does not gain a second vote through an additional office or an alt. Existing quorum, recusal, conflict review and one-person/one-vote rules remain. A councillor ordinarily serving Justice still needs case designation and conflict review; independent adjudication is not transferred to the Co-Guild Master by this direction.

The native ten-rank ladder is unchanged. No game, Discord, website, guild-bank or production permission is changed, and no live Council post is edited by this publication. Actual bank custody, withdrawal rights and confidential access remain separately authorized. This record fabricates no named signature, personal acceptance or software activation.
