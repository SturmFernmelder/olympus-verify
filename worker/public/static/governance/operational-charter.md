# Operational Charter — The Work of Olympus

## O1 The charge of service

Olympus is a fellowship of adventurers and a federation of guild households. Its strength rests on dependable people, clear promises and a welcome that survives a difficult raid night. The language of councils, ministers, warrants and emissaries gives our shared work a common identity. Every ceremonial title has a practical meaning: a person accepts a duty, receives a bounded mandate, keeps an intelligible record and answers for the result. No title purchases obedience outside that mandate.

The administration serves members who raid, fight in battlegrounds, craft, explore, level, roleplay or simply enjoy the company. A member's chosen activity determines the commitments they accept. Social members do not owe raid attendance. A crafter does not owe unpaid labour. A donation does not buy rank, an appointment, a favourable judgment or priority over published rules.

The adopted rules govern decisions from 13 October 2026. They do not retroactively create an offence, invent an old appointment or certify a feature as deployed. Actual game permissions, Discord roles, website access and financial custody each need the authorized operator and their own recorded implementation. Where the platform cannot implement a rule, the responsible officer records the limitation and uses a compatible procedure rather than claiming an imaginary control.

## O2 Four kinds of authority

Constitutional authority defines the common settlement and reserved decisions. A Council appointment defines a seat, mandate, eligible vote and term. A native rank classifies a verified character in the game. An operational permission allows a particular action in a particular system. These are separate records, reviewed together when a person begins or leaves service.

The Founder and Patron holds the reserved constitutional responsibilities described in the Charters. The High Council coordinates common policy, resources and portfolio performance. Each affiliated Guild Master commands their own guild within the common obligations. A minister works through that Guild Master and appointed emissaries; a ministry does not acquire ownership of a guild, its bank or its members.

Every instruction should make its source plain. An event order names the event leader and duration. A financial approval names the purpose and ceiling. A case direction identifies the authorized panel and scope. A technical release names the approved artifact and operator. An ambiguous instruction is clarified before an irreversible action. A request to exceed a mandate returns to the competent authority; urgency does not silently enlarge it.

## O3 The High Council and its offices

The High Council is a body of accepted appointees, not every character carrying the native rank label. Its office register identifies each eligible seat once, including any combined portfolios, acting arrangement and recusal. One person does not gain another vote through an alt or an additional office. A vacant office stays visibly vacant; attendance by an adviser does not appoint them or create a vote.

The two War Heads carry distinct PvP and Raiding portfolios. The Federal Treasurer carries the finance portfolio. The Ministers of Artisanry and Heraldry carry the crafting and community portfolios. Accepted High Councillors without another portfolio ordinarily serve the Council of Justice, subject to case designation and conflict review. Systems and Addons is a shared support division with a recorded lead; it is not an undeclared fifth ministry. Specialist service heads may advise the Council without becoming voting Council members unless separately appointed.

Before a meeting, the Chief of Staff circulates an agenda identifying the decision sought, the responsible portfolio, the evidence members may read and any financial or access implications. The Registrar records attendance, conflicts, the applicable quorum, the exact resolution, votes, reasons, implementation owner and review date. A confidential annex holds necessary protected evidence; the member-facing record states the decision and its practical effect without exposing that evidence. Silence is not recorded as assent.

The Council's monthly review asks what was promised, what was completed, what remains uncertain and what requires help. It reviews vacancies, service response times, the reconciled budget, access conflicts and overdue actions. It does not replace the event briefing or turn a volunteer's absence into an automatic disciplinary matter. A decision that cannot safely proceed records its hold and the condition needed to resolve it.

## O4 The executive household and the registry

Co-Guild Master, Vice Guild Master and Chief of Staff are separate appointments even when one person is expressly authorized to combine them. The Co-Guild Master coordinates the responsibilities actually delegated by the Founder. The Vice Guild Master provides the stated continuity cover. The Chief of Staff turns Council decisions into named actions, maintains the meeting rhythm and resolves collisions between portfolios. None inherits the Founder's reserved powers by implication.

The Secretary and Registrar maintains the authoritative office and decision registers. An appointment entry records office, holder's agreed public identity, issuing authority, acceptance, scope, reporting line, term, deputy cover and necessary permission review. The public directory displays only what the holder agreed to publish. A correction adds a dated amendment; it does not rewrite a past vote or replace a missing acceptance with a blank signature.

The office register distinguishes the existing website appointments of richwcampbell. as Co-Guild Master and Fernmelder as Treasurer from any wider governance warrant. Their existing status is not revoked by publication, and no further delegation is invented. The chart references for Maxran and Akafoxslayer remain references until individual appointment instruments are actually completed. Adoption of these rules does not appoint everyone pictured in an earlier chart.

## O5 The Department of War

The War Head for PvP maintains shared objectives, readiness standards and a network of local PvP emissaries. For each operation the local Guild Master confirms participation and names a Captain. The Captain briefs the objective, force, communication channel, rendezvous, withdrawal condition and code of conduct. Scouts report lawful in-game observations with their age and uncertainty. An exciting rumour is not a verified enemy position or grounds for an accusation against a player.

The War Head for Raiding maintains the common preparation standard, training resources and encounter review. Local Raid Leaders own their named events: roster confirmation, start and expected finish, published loot arrangements, consumable expectations, accessibility needs and reserve places. A change affecting loot or eligibility is announced before participants commit. A late operational change is explained; it cannot become a concealed penalty after the boss dies.

Raid assistants and Marshals hold specific tactical assignments. They may lead a group, manage a ready check or carry a briefing without gaining authority to promote, demote or spend from the bank. PvP Captains, Raid Leaders and Class Heads report lessons to their emissary after an operation. The useful record is brief: objective, result, obstacles, resource use and one or two improvements. It excludes shaming lists and unnecessary personal detail.

The Chief Strategist coordinates tested plans and encounter knowledge. The Head of Research states the game version, method and limits of each recommendation. The Scouting Lead filters relevant permitted observations. Together they offer advice to the responsible event commander; they do not issue a competing chain of orders during a pull. During an active event the named commander gives tactical direction, while a safety or conduct concern may always be escalated through its proper route.

## O6 The Federal Treasury and independent audit

The Federal Treasurer keeps the common budget, reconciles receipts and supports local Finance Emissaries. Each guild's resources remain under its own authorized custody unless a specific transfer is approved. The Treasurer publishes suitable aggregate balances and commitments, explains differences between available stock and earmarked resources, and does not conceal a withdrawal behind an accounting entry.

The established dues rule is one gold weekly, a fourteen-day new-member exemption and ninety-day ledger retention. The adopted accounting rule counts a verified member once in their designated home guild, without duplicate assessments solely for linked alts. Authorized people receive and record actual gold; the website neither debits a game balance nor proves an unobserved payment. Corrections preserve a reason and the original receipt reference. A disputed assessment is reviewed before a decision, and hardship handling remains confidential.

A requisition names the purpose, item or amount, event or beneficiary category, responsible requester and required date. The approver checks the budget and applicable threshold. The custodian records the actual transfer, and a different person reconciles it where independence is required. Related transfers are considered together so a substantial request cannot be split to evade review. No officer pays a private expense from the common bank merely because they hold a senior rank.

The Independent Bank Auditor reports findings to the unconflicted reviewing authority. Before examination, actual withdrawal, custody and approval rights over the assets must be reconciled with audit independence, including rights inherited from a native High Council preset. A title saying “auditor” does not neutralize an existing withdrawal permission. The auditor verifies records and reports an exception; an authorized custodian executes any correction. The person who approved or handled the transaction does not independently certify their own work.

## O7 The Association of Artisanry

The Minister of Artisanry maintains a practical picture of willing crafters, relevant specializations and supply needs. Members choose whether to list an offer. A request states the recipe, materials supplied, agreed fee or donation, delivery arrangement and responsible contact. No one is compelled to donate scarce materials, reveal private inventories or promise a recipe they do not possess.

Artisanry Emissaries reconcile local needs with War's event plan and Treasury's approved procurement. The Minister identifies shortages early, proposes alternatives and confirms who is actually making an item. A shared stock reservation is recorded before two teams are promised the same materials. A missed delivery is resolved through the agreed contact; it is not automatically a conduct finding.

The Quartermaster and Bank Steward handle only the stock entrusted to them under actual limits. They record issue, return, spoilage or other justified adjustment. Emergency reserves have a published local rule and an accountable approver. Crafting coordinators may recommend procurement, but their appointment does not grant bank access or make every guild bank a federal storehouse.

## O8 The Department of Heraldry

The Minister of Heraldry keeps Olympus understandable to its members: clear notices, a reliable calendar, recruitment information, an accessible welcome and a recognizable public voice. Heraldry distinguishes confirmed announcements, future plans and historical records. It obtains the relevant consent for names, pictures, streams and personal stories, and does not publish case evidence or private financial details as community news.

The Chief of Intake and Recruitment Lead maintains the application handoff. A local Recruiter explains the next step, the information needed, who is responsible and when a response can reasonably be expected. Verification is an actual identity process, not a favour from an officeholder. An accepted application, a game invitation, a character link and a staff appointment are different outcomes. Missing information remains missing; it is not rewritten as deceit.

The Events Lead coordinates the public calendar and avoids preventable clashes. Every event has an owner, start time and time zone, capacity, joining instructions, conduct expectations and cancellation contact. The Media and Communications Lead verifies the approved facts and format before publication. A reminder requires the organizer's actual consent and supported delivery path; a calendar entry alone is not permission to message someone.

The Academy and Training Lead arranges willing mentors, class clinics and approachable learning material. Each Initiate has a contact and a review date. A raid trial evaluates that activity without stripping established community membership. Learning support is not a compulsory play-hour programme. Welfare and Community Support offers a route to ask for help and refers serious concerns to the appropriate qualified or platform service rather than pretending a game office is a clinical authority.

## O9 Systems, Addons and the stewardship of tools

The Technical Lead coordinates website, AddOn, Discord and operating tools. Maintainers document the affected behaviour, review changes, retain an identifiable release and explain what an actual test established. A local test is not evidence of a live deployment; a successful deployment is not evidence of a real game action; a copied badge is not proof of authority.

Access is assigned to a named operator for a purpose and reviewed when that purpose changes. A Council title does not disclose credentials, grant production access or authorize browsing private records. Operators preserve configuration and recovery data, avoid unnecessary copies of personal information, and use the published retention and recovery controls. Publication alone neither changes those controls nor proves a live outcome. The current Native operating mode supports authenticated automatic serving-account erasure through bounded, guarded work; an account-specific hold does not close unrelated guild pages or ordinary maintenance. Completion covers live serving-account cleanup, not every copy. Unknown historical effects and separate recovery-copy custody retain their original purposes and limits. Reset preservation is distinct from an account owner's authenticated erasure request: the beta member/rank reset preserves manual leadership and Council roles, website appointments and the directory, while personal erasure follows the requesting account's attributable guild-role and data cleanup rules.

A release record names the exact artifact, responsible operator, checks, known limitations and observed deployment outcome. If the original answer is lost, the operator checks the original operation before considering another write. An unknown effect is not converted into success because a later screen looks normal. A recovery attempt keeps its own scope and cannot declare every physical backup clean without the required evidence.

## O10 Local command and the five emissaries

Each affiliated Guild Master records the local officer delegation and absence cover. Officers give clear instructions within that delegation and retain the member's correction and appeal routes. An Officer Alt is the verified alternate character of an officer, not another officeholder or Council vote. The guild's own permissions, bank limits and event controls are checked in the actual client by its competent operator.

Each participating guild names five correspondents: War for PvP, War for Raiding, Finance, Artisanry and Heraldry. A person may carry more than one duty if workload permits and conflicts are addressed. The emissary sends local needs to the portfolio and returns useful decisions to the guild. They do not use a ministry's name to override their Guild Master or make commitments beyond their warrant.

Guild Masters retain a direct constitutional relationship with the Founder and High Council. The chart's arrows express accountability, liaison or independent review; a crossing is not an extra reporting junction. Olympus I–X are directory places until affiliation and appointments are recorded. No extra guild, voting seat or command structure exists simply because a diagram has room for one.

## O11 Specialist service and the nine class heads

Class Heads for Warrior, Paladin, Hunter, Rogue, Priest, Mage, Warlock, Shaman and Druid offer class and specialization advice relevant to the actual game version. They arrange resources and willing mentors, coordinate with event leaders on needs, and distinguish preference from a demonstrated requirement. A Class Head cannot overrule published loot rules, demand account access or discipline a member by expertise alone.

Mentors agree an achievable introduction with the member: a point of contact, relevant resources and an opportunity to ask questions. Recruitment and Trial Officers own the actual review criteria and decision route. Local Social and Community Officers recognize participation beyond competitive events. Moderators act through their separate platform authority and case procedure, rather than deriving punishment rights from a cheerful social title.

Diplomatic Envoys maintain agreed external contacts through the Chief of Staff. They may explore cooperation and report terms. A binding alliance, resource commitment or public undertaking requires the written delegation and competent approval. Intelligence, Research, Diplomacy and Media begin as scoped appointments within the existing structure; they do not become new ministries by adopting grander names.

## O12 Justice, correction and appeal

The Council of Justice protects a fair process. A case has a responsible panel, defined question, relevant evidence, conflict review and an appropriate opportunity to respond. A person with a personal stake, prior involvement or incompatible custody recuses. Appeal reviewers did not decide the original matter. Necessary interim restrictions state their purpose, scope and review point; they are not a finding of guilt.

The decision record states the rule applicable at the time, material facts, uncertainty, reasons, action and review route. Authorized moderation staff execute the actual platform action. Publication gives the minimum useful process notice and protects participants' unnecessary personal information. An officeholder may disagree with a judgment through the recorded route, not by silently changing a bank right, removing a character or leaking private evidence.

A member may request correction of an inaccurate record without being labelled disloyal. The registrar preserves the original decision and the dated correction. An overturned decision triggers a review of dependent actions by their authorized owners. Honourary recognition, probation, an activity tag and a disciplinary finding remain different records. None creates a concealed extra native rank.

## O13 The ordinary rhythm of administration

Weekly, local leaders check intake, forthcoming events, time-sensitive member needs and resource custody. Fortnightly, portfolio heads and emissaries resolve cross-guild dependencies and share useful lessons. Monthly, the Council reviews the reconciled budget, office vacancies, risks, overdue actions and whether the service still merits its cost. These are service rhythms agreed with volunteers, not mandatory game attendance.

Each action has one accountable owner, a clear result, a due or review date and a handoff if the owner becomes unavailable. A deputy receives only the powers and activation condition actually written in the warrant. If both holder and deputy are absent, essential work is reassigned expressly; an unattended title is not permission for anyone nearby to act.

During a significant incident, the relevant lead states what is known, what is held, the next safe step and the next update. Tactical leaders protect the immediate event; technical operators protect systems and data; the unconflicted adjudicator owns the conduct question. These responsibilities can cooperate without merging their authorities. A short review records useful improvements and closes temporary powers at their original expiry.

## O14 Succession, departure and institutional memory

At the end of an appointment, the holder hands over open actions, approved records, resource custody and necessary access references. Credentials are not pasted into minutes or given to an informal successor. The competent operator reviews actual access; the registrar records the end of the mandate and the identity of the accepted replacement when one exists. Emeritus recognition carries no continuing permission unless a separate current mandate states it.

The first service cycle reviews these arrangements after six to eight weeks. The review asks whether offices helped members, whether duties can be combined, where a deputy is needed and whether records were proportionate. It does not fill every box for appearances. A new department needs demonstrated work, an accountable willing lead and the appropriate adopted amendment.

Historical instruments, archived announcements and old software releases remain labelled by their own date and status. The current adopted text is the governing reference. Templates have empty fields because an actual appointment must be made by actual people. No preprinted seal, generated signature or polished chart substitutes for that act.

## O15 Published Council responsibility portfolios

The authorized guild administration directed publication of these responsibility portfolios on 13 October 2026. They describe the work to coordinate; they do not represent a signed appointment instrument, a new native rank or a permission grant. Existing appointments and the local chains of command keep their separate status.

### Fernmelder — Treasury and guild operations

Discord: `fernmelder`

Fernmelder leads treasury and guild-bank administration: recording receipts, reconciling dues and fees against the adopted schedule, maintaining a clear ledger, and preparing understandable reports of balances, outstanding entries and discrepancies. Treasury records should distinguish money actually received in game from a promise, a proposed charge or an unconfirmed entry. Corrections must preserve an explanation of what changed and why. Fernmelder supplies records for independent audits and follows up their findings; independent review of funds in the Treasurer's custody belongs to a separate reviewer.

Fernmelder coordinates collection of approved dues and fees through the existing attended process and checks and reconciles those records. This responsibility creates no new charge or automatic collection mechanism.

Fernmelder coordinates with the AddOn and website developers on a reliable way to match in-game gold records to the website ledger. That work must make the origin and freshness of each record clear, allow an officer to resolve an unmatched receipt, and avoid representing a planned integration as an automatic transfer of gold.

The portfolio also includes ordinary non-hardcore guild administration, practical logistics and operational oversight. Fernmelder keeps administrative work moving, records hand-offs, identifies unresolved dependencies and brings matters requiring a wider decision to the appropriate Council or Guild Master. Financial authority follows the adopted bank permissions and approval process.

### freejam / Nikola — European coordination and guild administration

Discord: `freejam`

freejam / Nikola supports day-to-day administration and operational oversight for the non-hardcore guild community, with particular attention to European time zones. The work includes coordinating people, communicating practical arrangements and helping officers resolve routine organizational gaps.

Using the main guild's roster and members' stated availability, freejam helps form groups across the Olympus guilds and connects members with suitable organizers. Group formation should account for time zone, level, class needs and the activity's stated requirements. A proposed group is kept distinct from a confirmed roster or an accepted leadership appointment.

European scheduling needs and recurring coordination problems should be brought to the relevant officers or Council portfolio so that the wider organization can respond. Hand-offs should identify the next responsible person and any outstanding decision.

### Nomad — Hunter guidance, European activities and open-world PvP

Discord: `nomadraider`

Nomad develops Hunter guidance and supports class optimization and mentorship. This includes maintaining useful guides, helping members understand builds and play, answering class questions, and feeding recurring concerns into the wider class-advice work.

Nomad leads coordination of the main guild's European and early-time-zone activities and European-friendly event scheduling, under the designated guild and event authorities. This includes identifying practical play windows and connecting participants with the appropriate group leaders. The portfolio includes baseline guild organization where it helps those activities run reliably.

Open-world PvP and zone-control activities are organized as opt-in events with a clear purpose, meeting point, time and leader. Participants should know what they are joining. Plans and results should be coordinated with the Department of War and the affected guild leadership.

### Kryptiiq — Warlock guidance, raid support and community activities

Discord: `kryptiiq`

Kryptiiq supports Warlock guidance, class-roster coordination and strategy. The work includes helping players improve, identifying practical class needs for planned groups, and sharing relevant advice with raid organizers and the wider class-advice team.

Kryptiiq assists European raid leadership with preparation, coordination and follow-up under the event's designated raid leader. Class recommendations and proposed roster changes should be communicated clearly; final operational decisions remain with the responsible raid and guild leadership.

The portfolio also includes community PvP events and general operational logistics: helping arrange activities, communicating requirements, coordinating participants and handing off unresolved work to the appropriate organizer.

### Shared working rules

These portfolios divide work across Olympus; they do not replace each guild's Guild Master or officers. Class advice, a Council responsibility, a native guild rank and permission to operate a tool are separate matters.

Each portfolio holder should keep practical notes, make hand-offs understandable and report material unresolved issues to the appropriate leadership body. Personal cases, appeals and sensitive records belong in the designated private staff process. Public updates should explain activities and decisions without exposing private member records.
