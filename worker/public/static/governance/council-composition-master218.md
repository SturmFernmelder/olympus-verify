# The High Council — current portfolio register

Owner-directed Council supplement, 30 October 2026. Twelve reserved portfolios and the Guild Master as chair. This supplements the R7 institutional text; it records neither a newly ratified constitution nor issued appointments.

## Co-Guild Master / Hand of the Sovereign

Alliance executive coordination, continuity and cross-branch liaison within the accepted remit. Vice Guild Master and Chief of Staff are not added to this remit by implication.

Native association: High Council, rank 2, zero-based slot 1. Title II. Register key: office_19. The ten native ranks remain unchanged.

## Lord High Treasurer / Imperial Exchequer

Alliance budgets, receipts and financial stewardship, with custody, spending approval and independent audit kept separate.

Native association: High Council, rank 2, zero-based slot 1. Title III. Register key: office_86. The ten native ranks remain unchanged.

## Lord High Chancellor / Head of Justice

The Lord High Chancellor is the High Council’s individual judicial portfolio holder. The Council of Justice and Privy Court remain institutions with separately designated hearing panels; holding this office is not a permanent seat on every case or a power to judge one’s own enforcement decision. Panel service gives no blanket Council vote, case access or enforcement power; each participant still needs case designation, relevant access and conflict review.

Native association: High Council, rank 2, zero-based slot 1. Title II. Register key: office_101. The ten native ranks remain unchanged.

## Grand Marshal of PvE

The Grand Marshal of PvE directs alliance-wide raid preparation. This is the main raid portfolio on the High Council, distinct from the NA and EU Raid Leaders who command their own scheduled teams.

Native association: High Council, rank 2, zero-based slot 1. Title IV. Register key: office_97. The ten native ranks remain unchanged.

## Grand Marshal of PvP

The Grand Marshal of PvP coordinates voluntary battleground teams and world-PvP activity across Olympus. The office supplies strategy and reliable event leadership within a PvE guild; participation in PvP is never a condition of ordinary membership.

Native association: High Council, rank 2, zero-based slot 1. Title IV. Register key: office_98. The ten native ranks remain unchanged.

## High Inquisitor / Minister of Internal Security

## Head of Trading & Markets

A reserved High Council portfolio devoted solely to Auction House optimization, direct buy/sell negotiation and the market economy. The Treasurer authorizes capital and risk limits and retains custody; realized results and inventory are independently reviewable. No holder or access is granted by this reference.

Native association: High Council, rank 2, zero-based slot 1. Title III. Register key: office_96. The ten native ranks remain unchanged.

## Separation of powers

Treasury retains budget and custody; Trading has its own market portfolio within written capital and loss limits. Independent Audit may challenge either and stays outside the custodian’s line. Internal Security handles prevention and enforcement; the Chancellor assigns unconflicted hearings. Regional Raid Leaders and PvP Captains retain their actual event command. Branch Guild Masters retain local command and separately authorized invitation permissions.

No gold writ purchases a Council seat, vote, moderation power or bank withdrawal. A prior shared War Head or Council of Justice record is not silently copied to a new individual portfolio.
