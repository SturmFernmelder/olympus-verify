Privacy Policy

Olympus Verify · last updated 18 September 2026

Olympus Verify is a private bot serving a single Discord server: the community for the World of Warcraft guild “Olympus”. It is not offered to the public and is not installed in any other server. It exists to prove that a Discord account and an in-game character belong to the same person, so guild channels can be opened to guild members without an officer checking every name by hand.

What it stores

Only what that check needs: your Discord user ID and display name; your BattleTag and Battle.net account ID, if you choose to link one; the names of your characters in the guild, with the rank, level, class and guild notes the game's own guild roster already shows to every member; and a dated log of actions taken (role granted, role removed, application approved or denied) so officers can answer “why did this happen”.

It does not store your Blizzard password or your Discord password — it never sees either. It does not read, store or process your messages, your DMs, your email address, your IP address, your payment details, or anything at all about your activity outside this one guild.

Where it goes

Nowhere. Data lives in a Cloudflare D1 database owned by the guild's officers and is never sold, shared, transferred or used for advertising, training or analytics. There are no third-party trackers on this page. The only external services involved are Discord and Blizzard themselves, during the sign-in you initiate.

Who can see it

Guild officers, through the bot's own commands, can see which Discord account a character belongs to. Nobody else can. The bot's log channel is private to staff.

Getting rid of it

Ask any officer, or run /verify-status and follow the removal instructions there. Your records are deleted on request, and automatically once you leave the Discord server. Deletion is immediate and total; there is no archive.

Contact

The guild's Discord staff, in the server's help channel. This bot is run by volunteers, not a company.
