Age and eligibility

You must be at least 13 and meet any higher minimum age required by Discord or the law where you live. Using it

By verifying a character you confirm it is yours. Claiming someone else's character, sharing verification codes, or using the bot to gain access you were not given is grounds for losing guild and Discord access.

The guild site

By applying you confirm that what you wrote is true and that the account is yours. Joke, troll and abusive applications are permanently denied, and so is voting under several accounts. A leadership application goes on the voting board, where members can read it and vote on it, only when you tick the box that allows it; you can withdraw it at any time. Applying (first choice or backup), voting, writing someone in and entering a reserved name are requests and opinions, not promises: the guild's leadership decides roles and invitations.

The community pages. What you list in the directory or offer as crafting is yours to change or withdraw at any time while the page is on; a calendar answer can be given or changed until the event starts; a character name you enter is a claim until it matches the officers' record. The records the officers keep about you there — trials, restriction cases and their watch-lists, departure items, the dues ledger — are the guild's staff records; they do not by themselves grant or remove access, roles or membership. Guild dues are the guild's own arrangement: the ledger records what the officers confirm was paid, the mail reference is a matching aid and not proof of payment, and any question about a payment or a week is for the officers. The site's administrators have a rank-planning page whose drafts stay in their own browser; a draft is a proposal, never a change in the guild. The leadership directory of the Olympus guilds is a record the administrators keep; being listed there gives no powers. News notices are the administrators' announcements to the guild, and the News figures are approximate counts; neither is a promise.

A full guild. While Olympus I is full, a character whose code was confirmed keeps its place in the invite queue, and a refusal for lack of space costs it no invite attempt. A place in the queue is not a seat and promises no date, and names reserved on the site go first. The bot removes nobody to make room; officers may remove inactive characters.

Renamed characters. A character renamed because Blizzard required it, as a site administrator decides, has to be verified again and its owner has to apply again; unless another of the owner's characters is in the guild, the Guild Member role is held until the leadership accepts the new application and the character is verified again.

How it tells you about your access

The bot never sends direct messages. When something changes about your own access — your character is on the guild roster, you are back in the invite queue, or a seat was freed — it posts a short notice in the server's bot notices channel (#olympus-notices) and mentions you, so you get an ordinary notification. Only the welcome (your character is on the guild roster) is said in full; every other notice, including being back in the queue or a freed seat, and anything you might not want read out in front of the server, such as an invite that was declined or refused or an application that was decided, only says there is an update, and /verify-status shows you the detail privately. In that channel it posts nothing else — no announcements, no marketing — and it is rate-limited; whether a notice reaches you depends on the channel's permissions and on Discord. The bot also writes to the staff's own channels: its log lines, review cards and, where the officers switch it on, a daily digest of how many things wait for them, as counts only. You can mute the notices channel at any time; /verify-status always shows the same information on request.

Availability

Staff may change, pause or retire the bot at any time. Guild membership itself is decided by officers, not by this software; the bot only reflects the in-game guild roster.
