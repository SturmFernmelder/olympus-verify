Terms of Service

Olympus Verify · last updated 18 September 2026

Olympus Verify is provided free, as-is, with no warranty, by the volunteer staff of one World of Warcraft guild, for the use of that guild's members. It is not a commercial service and is not available for installation elsewhere.

Using it

By linking your account you confirm the character you are claiming is yours. Claiming someone else's character, sharing verification codes, or using the bot to gain access you were not given is grounds for losing guild and Discord access.

What it will message you about

The bot sends direct messages only about your own access: that your character was confirmed and your role is live, that your character left the guild, that a seat was freed, or that an application was decided. It sends nothing else — no announcements, no marketing, no bulk messages — and it is rate-limited so it cannot message people in bursts. You can close your DMs at any time without losing any access; /verify-status shows the same information on request.

Availability

Staff may change, pause or retire the bot at any time. Guild membership itself is decided by officers, not by this software; the bot only reflects the in-game guild roster.
