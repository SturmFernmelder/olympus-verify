## Aim, vision and objective

**Aim:** Give Olympus the resources to support progression, professions, events and members in difficulty.

**Vision:** A productive community able to sustain its shared activities without depending on one wealthy donor or one indispensable officer.

**90-day stretch objective:** Accumulate **1,000,000g of spendable, reconciled gold plus a separate 50,000g operating reserve**, after costs and outstanding obligations, over 90 operating days.

The goal is specific, measurable, relevant and time-bound. Its achievable component is **unvalidated** until real participation, profitable throughput, customer demand, working capital and available labour support it. The first 14 days are a feasibility pilot, not a recruitment promise.

The treasury exists to fund useful guild activity. A cash milestone does not itself approve spending, nor does it justify hoarding indefinitely. Council must separately approve the reserve and member-benefits budget.

## Accounting definition

Spendable gold is reconciled eligible cash less:

- Unpaid supplier, provider, commission and refund obligations.
- Customer deposits for unfinished work.
- Restricted in-game member-aid funds.
- The additional operating reserve.

Do not count pledges, loans, unsold inventory, unpaid invoices or estimated materials as spendable gold. Material gifts are inventory until converted into settled cash. A guild-funded match or transfer between guild accounts is not new income. An additional external sponsor transfer is a receipt only once. Keep member purchases separate from external customer demand and do not double-count the same internal gold movement.

Measure the target in **one agreed accessible treasury pool**. Report unrelated regions or realms separately; inaccessible gold elsewhere is not flagship spendable gold. Verify actual bank and authorised-custody limits before making a literal storage commitment. This programme does not approve extra accounts, custody workarounds or new bank privileges.

The target cash is 1,050,000g after costs and other exclusions: 1,000,000g available plus 50,000g reserved. Allocate the reserve once; it is not a weekly cost.

Every week, calculate:

`required remaining weekly net = max(0, 1,000,000g + reserve − current eligible cash) × 7 ÷ days remaining`

For this equation, eligible cash has already excluded obligations, unfinished-order deposits and restricted funds, but has not yet deducted the reserve. Do not subtract the reserve twice. If accounts do not reconcile, pause affected payments and sales, preserve records and investigate.

## Revenue and participation policy

### Voluntary contributions

Invite affordable fixed gifts or an optional share of personal trading profit. Explain the benefit being funded and allow a member to decline without penalty. Recognise gold, materials, work and knowledge separately. Do not use public wealth rankings or pay commissions for soliciting donations.

### Goods and permitted direct services

Pilot three promising offers using real repeat demand. Quotes must include materials, provider payment, selling fees, refunds and any coordination pay. Set funded purchasing and inventory limits.

Only directly fulfilled services whose actual Forever rules and advertising requirements have been checked may enter an authorised pilot. The cited Anniversary services clarification is not blanket Forever permission. This programme excludes loot auctions, organised boosting, matchmaking, brokerage, escrow and an umbrella multi-realm service network. Publishing an illustrative services revenue line is not approval of an actual catalogue.

A management or coordination fee is appropriate only for real work within a disclosed, opted-in and permitted service. It is not a charge for belonging to the guild, nor a hidden second settlement charge.

### Membership and justice

Ordinary guild membership and ordinary appeals remain free. Do not sell leadership, moderation powers, paid addon access, admissions immunity, guaranteed flagship placement, kick outcomes or punishment of another member. Judges and moderators have no fundraising target and no commission for sanctions.

Any historical conditional-clemency arrangement remains a separate independently reviewed case process and contributes **0g to every forecast**. This page does not solicit an appeal payment or sell a favourable outcome. Severe safety or platform-rule offences cannot be purchased away.

Existing patron cosmetics and Royal Writs remain paused/OFF and excluded from the economic forecast. A later cosmetic proposal must be separate from permissions, loot advantage and purchased office. No activation is implied here.

### In-game member aid

Restricted aid contributions need a separately accounted purpose, eligibility criteria and approval process. Do not describe an in-game fund as a real-world charity or tax-deductible donation. Do not condition aid on a recipient's prior donations or publish their private circumstances.

### Legitimate gold and consent

The programme uses legitimate in-game gold only: no real money, crypto, Twitch subscription exchange, illicit gold or coercion. A donation label does not make suspicious gold safe. Do not promise concealment or a way around an economy hold.

Publish aggregate results; obtain consent for personal recognition. Anonymous participation is possible. This programme creates no new identity retention, donor tracking, permissions or provider-dispatch system.

## Proposed incentives

- **Providers:** agreed payment for completed work, included in costs before Treasury margin.
- **Coordinators:** a proposed 10% of realised margin after materials and fulfilment, capped at 5% of sale price, paid only after delivery and settlement. A 100g order less 50g materials/fees and 15g fulfilment leaves 35g; the commission is 3.5g and Treasury retains 31.5g.
- **Gatherers:** funded purchase requests with clear prices and quantity caps, not uncapped promises.
- **Contributors:** optional recognition for reliable work, knowledge, mentoring, materials and gold without purchased authority or wealth ladders.
- **Departments:** recognition for useful member outcomes, net contribution, reliability and sound handover, including quality and workload.
- **Matching campaigns:** count only genuinely additional sponsor gold as new receipts; internal allocations are not fundraising income.

These are proposed pilot terms, not active payment rules. No officer approves their own payment. Large-payment thresholds require agreement before operation; the website does not grant a new limit or approval power.

## Roadmap

### Before Day 1

Confirm realm, treasury accessibility, actual bank/custody limits, authorised operators, opening statement and active-player baseline. Choose three permitted, provider-backed pilot offers; publish voluntary participation, payout and cancellation terms. Assign existing offices and fund a small pilot budget. Record the operating start date.

Acceptance evidence: named owners and backups, an accessible-pool definition, a funded catalogue and clear terms. No offer starts merely because this plan is visible.

### Short term: Days 1–30

1. Days 1–7: invite voluntary participation; fulfil small funded or customer-material orders; record settled sales and complete costs.
2. Days 8–14: measure repeat demand, contribution participation, margin, delivery time and officer hours; stop loss-making or unpermitted offers.
3. Day 14: compare measured capacity with the required remaining pace. Sign-ups and enthusiasm are not revenue evidence.
4. Days 15–30: expand only the best repeatable activities, build reserve and train backup operators.
5. Reconcile weekly and publish aggregate receipts, costs, exclusions and revised forecast.

Continue the million-gold attempt only while evidence supports it. Otherwise lower the target or extend the deadline without donor pressure or disciplinary fees.

### Mid term: Days 31–90

Days 31–60: concentrate on a small profitable catalogue, clear fulfilment windows and funded purchase limits. Track member and external receipts separately. Establish two consecutive weeks near the required net pace before calling the plan on track.

Days 61–90: reduce speculative stock; finish accepted work; settle provider pay, refunds and commissions. Publish the final cash, exclusions, reserve and spendable amount. A milestone requires eligible settled gold, not inventory or pledges.

### Long term: Months 4–12

Months 4–6: seek eight consecutive weeks in which recurring income covers recurring approved spending. Fund affordable repairs, consumables, profession support, events and aid through a separately approved benefits budget. Maintain backups and review donor/customer/provider/custody concentration.

Months 7–12: size reserves from actual essential spending and interruption risk. A proposed later reserve floor is 13 weeks of essential spending, subject to approval. Reduce handling time and errors without degrading member experience. Each quarter, retain, change or retire activities using profit, quality, workload, trust and member-benefit evidence.

## One programme, connected frameworks

- **SMART:** define the goal and deadline, measure settled gold, and validate rather than assume achievability.
- **OGSM:** objective of sustainable funding; stretch cash goal; strategies of voluntary support, demanded goods and permitted direct services; measures of net cash, participation, margin, quality and workload.
- **Business plan:** customers, offers, prices, complete costs, capacity, working capital, risks and benefits.
- **Strategic plan:** choose the channels worth funding and excluded activities.
- **Action plan:** a named existing officer, deadline, funded resources and acceptance evidence per task.
- **Operational plan:** quote, accept, fund, fulfil, settle, reconcile and handle exceptions consistently.
- **OKRs/KPIs:** a small shared scorecard, not parallel committees or extra authority.

### OKRs

1. **Build a useful treasury:** Day-14 feasibility decision; current settled-cash forecast; pursue the stretch milestone only while measured capacity supports the remaining pace.
2. **Earn trust:** reconcile weekly; publish aggregate accounts; independently review material discrepancies; keep membership, appeals and discipline independent of payment.
3. **Create earning capacity:** positive realised margin, a proposed ≥95% on-time target, repeat demand, funded capacity, trained backups and weekly workload checks.

### Weekly KPIs

Cash and spendable funds; reserve and unpaid obligations; net growth and forecast; contributions/goods/services separately; internal versus external receipts; fully costed realised margin; unique participation and repeat customers; on-time delivery, errors and refunds; officer hours and concentration; complaints, discrepancies and useful benefits delivered.

All proposed quality targets are targets, not claims about current performance. Do not publish private wealth or case details.

### SWOT

Strengths: guild identity, leadership and profession coordination. Weaknesses: no reported starting capital and unverified population, margins, demand, pool limits and labour. Opportunities: repeat consumable needs, expertise, dependable fulfilment and voluntary support. Threats: launch scarcity, price swings, illicit gold, access interruptions, concentration, burnout and loss of trust.

### TPS and Lean Six Sigma

TPS means **Toyota Production System**. Produce against demand, limit work in progress, standardise handovers and stop an affected process for quality, rules, accounting or payment failure. Remove waiting, rework and double handling without harming members.

Use DMAIC practically: **Define** the need and boundaries; **Measure** price, complete cost, time, demand and errors; **Analyse** the actual constraint; **Improve** one small funded batch; **Control** the procedure only while results remain acceptable. More recruitment does not necessarily solve a shortage of buyers, materials, skills, cash or officer time.
