This 23 October 2026 supplement records the owner's public PvE Alliance direction, the chosen B ladder with High Council in Slot 2, Treasurer in Slot 3 (zero-based index 2) and Officer in Slot 4, the core Council offices of Guild Master, Co-Guild Master and Treasurer, and the cosmetic recognition rules below. The Court is the public service of the guild; the Privy Council is an advisory gathering, not a second legislature or a source of undisclosed power. These names confer no governmental status, real-world nobility or authority over another person's account.

## RC2 The core Council and portfolio service

The core leadership Council consists of the offices of Guild Master, Co-Guild Master and Treasurer. Guild Master remains responsible for the individual guild and protected game configuration. Co-Guild Master coordinates implementation and continuity within the accepted delegation. Treasurer safeguards the bank, receives and reconciles in-game gold, and reports resource decisions. Combining offices does not create another person or another vote. An appointment's acceptance, voting eligibility, recusal and actual permissions remain separately recorded; publication is not an executed warrant.

War and Raiding, Justice, Artisanry, Heraldry, Welfare, Systems and the emissary network remain accountable portfolios. A department reports an identified owner, current remit, useful output, next action and unresolved dependency. Ministers cannot use a courtesy title to command another guild or spend its bank. Emissaries carry approved information in both directions and distinguish confirmed decisions from proposals and local preferences.

## RC3 Council of Justice and reasoned review

Justice receives a concise complaint, identifies the rule and evidence at issue, screens conflicts, gives the affected person a fair opportunity to answer, and records a reasoned outcome and review route. Private safety records stay with authorized reviewers. Public summaries omit unnecessary identities and case details. A donor, broadcaster, officer or councillor receives neither immunity nor a purchased result. Urgent protective action must identify its purpose, custodian, review deadline and how an error can be corrected.

## RC4 Royal decrees and delegated instruments

A decree is a reasoned guild decision, subordinate to the Charter. Its complete record comprises an instrument identifier, edition, approving authority, exact rule or delegation, reason, effective time and time zone, review or expiry, implementation owner, publication scope, conflict declaration and superseded instrument. An emergency decree states why ordinary notice was unavailable and provides the earliest practicable unconflicted review. No decree may order platform abuse, disclosure of private credentials or bypass of erasure safeguards.

Appointment documents are complete **unexecuted instruments**, not draft promises of authority. The registrar must capture the office key and label, appointing authority, accepted holder account, scope, reporting and coordination duties, term, recusal, deliverables, review date, actual permission inventory, holder acceptance and the approving record. Until those real facts are supplied, status is unexecuted and no holder, signature or permission is invented. Revocation records identify the original instrument, reasons, effective time, custody handover and separately confirmed access removal. A source-page date cannot substitute for any of these acts.

## RC5 Crafters, artisans and the workshop

The Workshop connects willing crafters to members seeking recipes, materials or advice. A listing states profession, recipe, optional skill claim, last update and whether the information is self-declared or independently confirmed. A directory listing, rare recipe or title is not proof of character ownership. No one shall publish private trades, real-money prices, account credentials or another member's location or schedule. A crafter sets availability voluntarily and may withdraw a listing; stale or unavailable entries are labelled rather than presented as promises.

The Minister of Artisanry coordinates shared projects and keeps an intake list with recipe, materials, responsible volunteer and expected handoff. Procurement from a common bank requires the existing independent approval and receipt rules. An Artisan, Crafter or Quartermaster title does not itself grant withdrawal, officer chat or access to private contribution records.

## RC6 Treasury Tiers and Tithe Payers

Cosmetic Tithe Roles recognize **additional, voluntary in-game gold**, separately from ordinary weekly dues and hardship decisions. No real money, tokens, purchases outside the game or automatic debit is part of this system. Members mail the additional gold to Fernmelder with a TITHE reference. The admitted Treasurer or an independently authorized reviewer verifies the actual receipt and records its purpose in the existing contribution process. A player's mail reference or claim alone is not payment proof. Ordinary dues, a waived obligation and an unclassified historical receipt do not become cosmetic credit automatically.

The owner’s chosen schedule applies to verified additional contributions within each ninety-day season: Tithe Payer requires at least 25 additional gold, Patron of the Treasury at least 100, and Benefactor of Olympus at least 500. At most the highest currently qualified tier is shown, as cosmetic colour only, and every award lapses at that season’s end. There is no permanent donor rank or rolling lifetime credit; a new season needs its own eligible receipts. The title identifies the tier without publishing a balance. The website never publishes a member's exact amount, receipt, hardship status, private ledger or payment correspondence. A person may decline public recognition without losing credit or guild standing.

Recognition is cosmetic only. It gives no channels, native rank, Council seat or vote, bank right, raid/loot priority, admission preference, exemption from rules or access to confidential information. The existing one-gold weekly dues rule, fourteen-day exemption, hardship review and ninety-day ledger retention remain separate. Staff must not retain a private receipt longer merely to keep an expired cosmetic award visible.

An award decision records the chosen tier, qualifying period, current holder consent, reviewer and correction/review date through the existing admitted controls. An administrator selects only a configured cosmetic Discord role whose permission bits are zero and which is below the bot; the change is reviewed separately from any membership or staff role. This publication does not create a Discord role or dispatch a grant. Until that narrow writer is qualified and configured, staff may record an unexecuted award decision and display no claimed live Discord effect. A lost provider answer is checked under the original operation; it is never a reason to issue a replacement grant blindly.

Voided, reversed or misattributed receipts require a review of the award. A correction records the reason without exposing payment details, obtains any required consent, and reconciles only the affected cosmetic role. Expiry or erasure removes public recognition under the existing account safeguards; it never removes guild membership as a punishment. No eligibility is inferred from a deleted ledger or an unconfirmed external effect.

## RC7 VIP, broadcaster and correspondence tiers

VIP, Broadcaster, Content Creator, Honorary and Emeritus styles recognise service or an accepted public role. They do not outrank the native ladder or buy an appointment. A broadcaster's local Stream Shield is a presentation preference and cannot change server access or account-data rights. Public-facing names require the same current membership, account standing and publication treatment as other appointments. A Town Crier or Lead Correspondent distinguishes guild decisions, original reporting and attributed external sources, dates corrections, and publishes only to the approved destination. Optional Discord publication must not expose private drafts or the private officer digest.

Sign in through Discord, review the guild rules, select an activity suited to your time, and use the current verification instructions for the character you intend to link. An application, a vote and an invite are separate steps. Check the evidence timestamp on capacity information and do not treat a missing guild report as zero members. ## RC9 Officer Operational Guide

Before acting, identify the actual authority and current account/character, inspect the latest original record, check conflicts and obtain the required publication consent. Use the admitted website controls rather than a copied user identifier or a stale browser result. Record the decision and owner, then distinguish a saved decision from an external effect that has actually completed. Never promote a character to make a signer test pass, turn an uncertain DELETE into a claimed erasure, or retry an unknown provider operation as a new grant. Verify handoffs, preserve original failures and request an unconflicted review when authority or outcome is uncertain.

Treasury officers reconcile receipts against the actual in-game transfer; correspondents verify event times and source links; crafters label self-declared offers; moderators protect case details; Systems preserves deployment, recovery and privacy guards. The same person may perform several duties but cannot independently approve their own custody transaction or case. A sensible operational report names what changed, its evidence, its limitations and the next review time.
