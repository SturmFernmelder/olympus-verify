## Strict finance answer

**No complete automatically verifiable per-sender ledger on Fern Melder's PC is established by this archive alone without an authorized recipient observation/export/relay path.** The keeper must actually observe the native intake. In-game addon communication can avoid an external watcher for some online aggregates/payer acknowledgements, but GF intentionally omits payer details, FR goes to the payer, and SavedVariables are local and flush only at reload/logout/quit. A collector-signed export would attest to that collector's assertion; it would not by itself make editable local data an independent server attestation. Preserve Fern Melder as our canonical collector/whisper target. The separate source default Pyralis Ashandar/Andarai identifies the addon Treasurer pins and does not prove our operational character was renamed.

- Preserve membership_only=true, Native E2, exact raw own-guild/realm scope and existing HMAC protocol; canonical Prime aliases affect affiliation/display/admission only at the designated seam. Never rewrite historical/raw signed scope bytes.
- Dedicated Knight invite key version: `launch-knight-invite-v2`, source-owned `nativeInvite` observation, <=60s old/+5s future, GUID/name/code/rawscope MAC. Worker independently rechecks current linked Discord account and one of the exact nine Asmongold Knight-or-higher roles. This authorizes only a bounded invite queue, never a membership role grant.
