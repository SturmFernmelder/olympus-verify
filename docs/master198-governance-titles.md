# Title I — The Sovereign & Royal Decrees

**The Crown bears the charge of service before it bears the seal of command.** These five Titles are the owner's 25 October 2026 expansion of the current R7 reader. They give the guild's existing constitutional settlement a fuller statutory and roleplay expression. R7's original adoption remains 13 October 2026; this publication records no new Council vote, Founder signature, individual warrant or game permission. If a provision conflicts with the joint Charters, the Guild Statute, the published privacy policy or a platform rule, the controlling provision prevails and the registrar publishes the discrepancy for correction. More protective notice, confidentiality and independent-review requirements continue to apply.

Module names identify the intended divisions of work in the wider Olympus addon ecosystem. They are a map for members and implementers, not a declaration that every named module, signing facility or protected action is installed, adopted by the upstream publisher, configured or qualified in a real client. A signed record can establish an authentic statement only within its verified trust and scope; it cannot manufacture a lawful appointment or turn a software flag into constitutional consent. Current public direction is **PVE - Alliance**; the launch realm is **not announced**. Historical PvP beta records retain their dates, and optional PvP activities retain their proper offices.

## Article I.1 — The Sovereign's charge and the Guild Master's executive office

### Section I.1.1 — The Crown within the Charter

(a) The King or Sovereign is the ceremonial style of the Founder and Patron. The Sovereign guards the common settlement, receives the counsel of the High Council and gives reasons when exercising a reserved decision. In the hall of Olympus, the first obligation of the Crown is to preserve the fellowship: equal rules for the celebrated and the quiet, room for the casual player beside the raider, and no office purchased by favour or gold.

(b) The Guild Master is the executive custodian of the individual guild's actual roster and protected game configuration, subject to affiliation and the common Charter. The offices may be held by the same person without merging all powers. A royal style, native rank or website label is not authority to read private casework, seize a member's account or command an unaffiliated guild. `King.lua` is the named executive interface in this codex; its presence or absence never changes that boundary.

### Section I.1.2 — Reserved and delegated decisions

(a) Constitutional ratification, confirmation of affiliation and other expressly reserved decisions remain with the authority identified in the Charters. Ordinary administration may be delegated by a complete warrant naming its holder, subject matter, financial ceiling, reporting duty, effective period and exclusions. A deputy receives only those enumerated powers. Neither silence nor an empty office creates a general right of succession.

(b) Before implementing a consequential executive act, the responsible operator checks the current instrument, actual person and relevant platform capability. The register distinguishes approved, unexecuted, pending, confirmed and uncertain effects. A command prepared in an addon, an Admin selection and an accepted motion are different events; none is described as a completed external change without its own evidence. No decree authorizes credential sharing or overrides the separate account-owner erasure process.

## Article I.2 — The making and publication of royal decrees

### Section I.2.1 — Form of an edict

(a) A decree prepared under the `Decree.lua` division shall bear a unique instrument reference and edition; its competent issuer; the enabling rule; the exact operative words; reasons; affected branches; adoption and effective times with a stated time zone; expiry or review date; conflicts; implementation owner; and the instrument it varies or supersedes. Annexes form part of the approved edition only when identified in it. A flourish, crest or proclamation cannot supply a missing operative field.

(b) The registrar publishes the member-readable text before ordinary commencement and keeps sensitive implementation details in an access-limited annex. Ordinary Council measures receive the existing seven calendar days' notice; a decree requiring that process cannot evade it by changing its name. Members must be able to determine which edition governs. A correction identifies the earlier reference and the precise replacement, preserving the old record as superseded rather than quietly rewriting the law.

### Section I.2.2 — Promulgation across the banners

(a) The herald transmits the same approved wording to every affected affiliated branch and records destination, edition and publication outcome. A branch may add a clearly separated local explanation, but may not disguise a different obligation as the common decree. A failure to deliver is reported as such; a queued message or lost provider reply is not a proved announcement. Automatic mentions remain disabled unless that exact audience and action have their own authority.

(b) Where an edict needs implementation in the website, bot or client, the public notice states the policy's effective date separately from technical availability. A reserved feature stays unavailable while its launch gates are closed. No citizen is disciplined for failing to use an unpublished or unavailable mechanism. The registrar records an accessible alternative and the responsible contact while delivery or accessibility problems are resolved.

## Article I.3 — Seals, keys and authenticated alliance instruments

### Section I.3.1 — What a cryptographic seal attests

(a) The `Keys.lua` division concerns the custody and verification of the approved public-key trust records used by its actual protocol. An alliance-wide signed edict must bind the complete approved payload and its purpose, issuer, edition, scope, lifetime and supersession reference. Verification checks the exact bytes, allowed signer set, current validity and any applicable replay or revocation rule before displaying an instrument as authenticated. An unrecognized key, incomplete envelope or failed check is unverified, never repaired by guessing an identity.

(b) Authentication proves only what the verified protocol can establish. A valid signature is not evidence of a Council quorum, a bank transfer, an invite or the truth of a disputed allegation. The registrar must join it to the separate approving record and the issuer's current mandate. The public copy may show the instrument reference and verification state; secret keys, recovery material and private credentials never enter a decree, screenshot, news post or court bundle.

### Section I.3.2 — Custody, compromise and capability boundaries

(a) A key custodian keeps a named purpose, permitted signer role, authorized device or custody arrangement and an independent recovery contact. Suspected compromise calls for the narrowest safe pause and an attributed notice naming the affected public key or instrument range without exposing secrets. Rotation and revocation require their own competent authorization, validation and handover record; this text grants no power to mint a key, replace a trust root or erase original evidence.

(b) Governance signing, the existing authority-paste workflow and Olympus Link's Ed25519 peer verification have different purposes and trust boundaries. A key or successful check from one is not silently accepted for another. Link remains OFF until its complete configuration and separate qualification are approved. Named upstream modules and Draft PR #72 remain publisher-adoption dependencies where applicable. No statement in this Title certifies adoption in the official CurseForge addon or universal interoperability.

## Article I.4 — Emergency powers and the watch of the Crown

### Section I.4.1 — Necessity and the narrowest measure

(a) An authorized officer may invoke an emergency measure only for an immediate credible risk to people, entrusted assets or operation of the guild's services. The record describes the concrete risk, supporting observations, affected capability, start time, initiating officer and why ordinary notice would not suffice. Good-faith criticism, a complaint, an appeal, declined attendance or unpaid voluntary recognition is never an emergency ground. A troubling rumour alone is not a finding of guilt.

(b) Choose a reversible restriction limited to the risk. A local channel hold does not imply a cross-branch ban; a suspected key problem does not authorize a roster purge. Preserve the original evidence and tell the affected person the safe summary and review route as soon as practicable. Safety may require redaction, but a secret accusation cannot become a permanent sanction without the fair process in Title II.

### Section I.4.2 — Expiry and independent review

(a) The existing twenty-four-hour expiry governs every temporary emergency measure. Before expiry, an unconflicted authorized reviewer may grant a written renewal stating continuing necessity, precise scope and a new expiry no more than twenty-four hours later. The initiating officer cannot self-renew. Independent review begins within the existing forty-eight-hour limit, even if a short measure has already ended; expiry does not excuse examination of an error.

(b) The Crown may call the council bell and seek urgent advice, but cannot use an emergency to appoint a Council, amend the constitution permanently, approve substantial private spending or destroy a complaint. A continuing dispute goes to ordinary proceedings with their notice and evidence protections. Every extension has a next review point; a chain of automatic renewals or an unexplained indefinite hold is forbidden. Technical inability to undo an effect is reported honestly and escalated for attended correction.

## Article I.5 — The royal register, succession and accountability

### Section I.5.1 — The clerk's evidentiary duty

(a) The register links each edict to its proposal, competent approval, exact edition, publication, implementation and later review. It distinguishes a contemporary original from a transcript, a member's report and an inference. Corrections append an explanation and preserve provenance. Public entries contain enough reason to understand the decision without exposing unnecessary account identifiers, private financial evidence or protected case details.

(b) The clerk circulates ordinary minutes within the established seven-day period and records a correction visibly when a participant disputes accuracy. Missing records remain missing; an attractive seal or later recollection is not substituted for an absent signature. Disagreement over the register goes to an unconflicted reviewer. Its service to the Crown is fidelity to the record, not embellishment of a preferred story.

### Section I.5.2 — Vacancy, recusal and the end of a mandate

(a) Resignation, expiry, lawful revocation or a declared conflict ends the affected delegation according to its instrument. An acting warrant identifies the temporary holder and end condition; no title alone appoints a successor. The outgoing custodian hands over open work, approved records and unresolved effects. Actual access removal is a separate operator task with a confirmed or uncertain outcome, never an assumption inferred from a changed chart.

(b) A Sovereign personally interested in a case does not determine its facts. The independent recommendation, any reserved constitutional response and its reasons remain separate entries. Members retain the right to seek correction and appeal without retaliation. The golden banner is a promise of accountable service, not immunity from the laws beneath which it flies.

# Title II — The High Council, Co-Guild Master & Privy Court

**Let no judgment be louder than its evidence, and no office broader than its warrant.** `Court.lua`, `Acts.lua` and `Moderation.lua` name the deliberative, instrument and enforcement divisions of this Title. The Court is the guild's service of recorded counsel and fair hearings; the Privy Court or Privy Council is an advisory style, not a second secret legislature. The following procedure elaborates existing constitutional safeguards without issuing a new appointment or declaring an unqualified moderation mechanism live.

## Article II.1 — Composition, mandate and co-signatory acts

### Section II.1.1 — Seats and executive coordination

(a) Guild Master, Co-Guild Master and Treasurer are the core Council offices in the current owner direction, supported by the retained portfolio mandates. High Council remains native Slot 2 in the owner-selected ten-rank recommendation; Treasurer remains native Slot 3, while the Treasury office and its acceptance are separate records. Co-Guild Master is an appointment, not an eleventh native rank. Holding several offices creates one person and at most one eligible vote.

(b) The Co-Guild Master coordinates delivery, absence cover and local Guild Master liaison within the accepted warrant. It does not confer unlimited constitutional succession or a right to change another branch's protected configuration. The registrar records actual voting eligibility, vacancies, conflicts and acting terms before treating an office as a participating seat. A website appointment remains distinct from a still-unexecuted governance warrant; neither is silently cancelled or enlarged by this codex.

### Section II.1.2 — The complete act and its co-signatures

(a) An act prepared in the `Acts.lua` division identifies its authority, exact text, affected guilds, financial or access consequences, approving procedure and implementation owner. Where two approvals are required, the record names both independent approving persons and what each approved. One holder using two office titles, a duplicate key or an alternate character cannot satisfy independence. A request for review is not an approval, and a rendered countersignature field is not an executed signature.

(b) Approved-paste and similar authority exports transmit only their defined, verified record to a compatible adopted client. They do not appoint missing holders, constitute a Council vote or apply game permissions by themselves. Stale, malformed or disputed authority records stay unaccepted until corrected through the competent source. Implementation evidence identifies the actual client and configuration; a source demonstration is labelled as such.

## Article II.2 — Deliberation, quorum and reasons

### Section II.2.1 — Notice and the council table

(a) Ordinary meetings give seven calendar days' notice and an intelligible agenda; urgent meetings give at least twenty-four hours where practicable and record why shorter notice was necessary. The clerk establishes the current total of non-vacant voting seats and the eligible participants. A matter cannot be slipped into a different heading to deny a person reasonable notice. Sensitive annexes may be limited to competent reviewers while a non-sensitive description remains available.

(b) Quorum is more than half of the total non-vacant seats, with at least three eligible, unconflicted participants. Recusal does not lower either the denominator or that floor. Where fewer than three can act, an unconflicted appointing authority seeks an independent panel recommendation; the panel acquires no Council votes and cannot circumvent quorum. Discussion without quorum is advice, expressly recorded as non-binding.

### Section II.2.2 — Decision and publication

(a) Ordinary motions require a majority of eligible votes cast at a quorate meeting. Abstentions are recorded without becoming affirmative votes; a tie fails and the chair has no second casting vote. A written resolution uses the same eligibility, notice and quorum requirements and states a closing time. Constitutional recommendations retain the two-thirds-of-total-non-vacant-seats rule followed by Founder ratification. A simple majority cannot disguise a constitutional change as an ordinary act.

(b) Minutes name the proposal, authority, conflicts, attendance, vote, reasons, implementation owner and review date, and are circulated within seven days. A public summary distinguishes a decision from a recommendation, an executed action from a pending one and verified facts from unresolved claims. Case evidence and personal payment details remain private. Members may request correction of a material recording error without being accused of disloyalty.

## Article II.3 — Officer conduct and the independence of Justice

### Section II.3.1 — Duties of an officer

(a) An officer shall use entrusted powers for the published purpose, disclose a personal or financial interest, keep private material within authorized channels and refrain from threats, favouritism, impersonation and retaliation. A rare recipe, large donation, audience or friendship with leadership grants no exemption. The officer must identify when speaking for an office and when expressing a personal preference; ceremonial rank cannot turn that preference into law.

(b) A conflict includes being complainant, material witness, recipient, custodian under review or a close participant in the disputed event. Disclose it before receiving the merits bundle and withdraw from investigation, decision and appeal as appropriate. An unconflicted authority records the replacement. Necessary factual assistance may be requested without restoring a vote. The person whose conduct is examined cannot select a favourable fact-finder or approve their own evidence exclusion.

### Section II.3.2 — Evidence stewardship and standard of proof

(a) The case owner records the source, date, context and integrity limits of each exhibit. Original messages or logs are preferred to cropped images; testimony is identified as testimony, and a technical observation is bounded by what was actually visible. A missing record, offline player, stale roster or unsuccessful network request proves neither misconduct nor absence of an entitlement. No private token, recovery key or irrelevant account information is requested as an exhibit.

(b) Ordinary contested facts are decided on the balance of probabilities: the panel explains why the supported account is more likely than not. A lasting exclusion or finding of serious dishonesty additionally requires clear, cogent and corroborated evidence proportionate to its gravity; an uncorroborated rumour cannot meet that protection. Emergency necessity is a separate provisional risk assessment, not a lower route to a final conviction. The panel records uncertainty and the evidence it could not obtain.

## Article II.4 — Complaints, hearings and the trial record

### Section II.4.1 — Intake and a meaningful answer

(a) A designated officer acknowledges a received complaint within three working days, identifies an unconflicted case owner and states the next expected step. Mediation is voluntary and appropriate only where safe; serious safety allegations need not pass through it. Intake records the alleged rule, approximate event, requested remedy and available sources, avoiding a second public allegation thread. The complainant is not promised a result before the evidence is reviewed.

(b) Before a consequential judgment, the subject receives the alleged breach, useful evidence summary, possible consequence and at least seven calendar days to respond, unless a narrow emergency measure is independently justified. Private sources may be protected by an intelligible summary and questions through the case owner. A decision cannot rest solely on undisclosed material. Reasonable extensions for availability or accessibility are recorded with a new date, without requiring a person to disclose unnecessary medical details.

### Section II.4.2 — The sitting of the Court

(a) A hearing may be written or a private meeting with an agreed record. The chair states the issues, confirms recusals, receives each side's relevant account and permits questions through a respectful procedure. No compelled public spectacle or trial by stream is permitted. Witnesses distinguish what they saw from what they were told. Recording or publication requires the applicable consent and policy; declining publicity is not evidence against a party.

(b) The written outcome addresses each material allegation, the rule in force at the time, evidence accepted or rejected with reasons, findings, proportionate remedy, actual enforcing authority and appeal deadline. The panel gives an expected decision date and explains any delay before it elapses; absent a more protective case arrangement, it aims to decide within fourteen calendar days after the response period closes. Delay never converts an unproved allegation into guilt or silently extends an emergency hold.

## Article II.5 — Penal measures and the Sentry Hold code

### Section II.5.1 — Proportionality and a ladder of remedies

(a) The `Moderation.lua` division implements only a separately authorized measure within actual platform capability. A corrective explanation, agreed restitution or warning should be considered before an activity restriction where the risk and gravity permit. A sanction identifies the conduct addressed, its scope and duration, review condition and person authorized to end it. A raid selection decision about current readiness is not silently repurposed as a community-wide disciplinary ban.

(b) The Court considers seriousness, demonstrated harm, intent where evidenced, recurrence and reasonable mitigation. It does not punish poverty, a waived obligation, refusal of optional tithe recognition, lawful criticism or a good-faith appeal. Native rank, directory standing, moderation safety roles and account privacy rights are separate capabilities. A decision affecting one does not authorize a general account purge or an invented rule against all linked characters.

### Section II.5.2 — Sentry Hold as protection, never a sentence in disguise

(a) Penal / Sentry Hold (Timeout) is a bounded protective or disciplinary label only when a competent decision gives it actual scope. A provisional Sentry Hold follows Title I's twenty-four-hour expiry, independent renewal and forty-eight-hour review. A final time-limited restriction requires the hearing record and stated end date. The label alone applies no sanction and creates no new native rank. An absent or inaccessible implementation control remains unavailable, not presumed effective.

(b) The operator records the original request and actual acknowledgement. If an external answer is lost, preserve the original operation and check its permitted status; do not blindly send a replacement restriction or removal. Unknown stays unknown. A technical failure cannot lengthen the sentence by stealth. Corrective access changes require their competent operator, and successful removal of a website label does not prove a game or Discord permission has been restored.

## Article II.6 — Appeals, correction and restoration

### Section II.6.1 — A new ear for the petition

(a) The subject may appeal within fourteen calendar days of receiving the written decision for unfair procedure, material new evidence, an unreasonable finding or a disproportionate consequence. An uninvolved reviewer acknowledges within seven days and supplies an expected decision date. The original investigator, decision-maker and any materially interested person do not hear the appeal. If an impartial body is unavailable, the competent unconflicted authority appoints one rather than recycling the same panel.

(b) The appeal may uphold, vary or return the matter for reconsideration and gives reasons on the grounds raised. No automatic increase in sanction follows an appeal. A safety measure during review still needs its own lawful justification, expiry and review; lodging an appeal is not that justification. The reviewer states what relief is effective immediately and what depends on an attended platform correction.

### Section II.6.2 — Clearing the record and learning from error

(a) A corrected finding is appended to the original case and reflected in any member-readable summary without needless republication of the allegation. The registrar identifies recipients of the erroneous operational instruction so that authorized operators can reconcile its effects. Restoration of a role, invite ability or bank permission is separately confirmed. A case marked corrected is not represented as proof that every outside copy or external consequence disappeared.

(b) Justice reviews recurring procedural failures and recommends a prospective rule or training change. Case retention follows its actual purpose and published privacy policy; a court title is not a blanket audit-retention exemption. Authenticated serving-account erasure and narrow safety, unresolved-effect or recovery custody retain their own rules. The Court safeguards both the right to be heard and the right not to have private evidence turned into a permanent public memorial.

# Title III — The Imperial Treasury, Guild Bank & Tithe Ledger

**The vault is held in trust for the fellowship; its keys are a burden of proof.** `Treasury.lua`, `Dues.lua` and `Bank.lua` name the resource, obligation and custody divisions of this Title. Imperial and federal are roleplay styles for a voluntary community treasury. They create no real-world tax, investment, property title or promise of repayment. This Title preserves the existing manual in-game receipt process and does not turn a ledger into a gold collector, bank operator or automatic sanction engine.

## Article III.1 — The Treasurer's fiduciary charge

### Section III.1.1 — Office, custody and independent oversight

(a) The Treasurer safeguards entrusted guild resources, maintains a comprehensible ledger, proposes useful appropriations and reports material uncertainty promptly. The current named Treasury appointment is distinct from a native character rank and from actual bank permissions. A warrant specifies assets, custody powers, financial ceiling, deputy and reporting line. An empty form, public office name or selected role does not authorize a withdrawal.

(b) No person independently approves their own payment or audits assets while retaining their withdrawal, custody or transaction-approval rights. The documented High Council preset's actual rights must be reconciled before describing an auditor as read-only. A different title is insufficient. An unconflicted operator establishes the compatible access arrangement and records it; this publication does not change anyone's rank or bank permissions.

### Section III.1.2 — Care, segregation and reporting

(a) Identify common resources separately from a custodian's private property and from restricted donations for an accepted purpose. Keep only records necessary to show the transaction, authority and reconciliation; secret account access is never a treasury record. Reconcile routine custody weekly and obtain the existing monthly independent review. If a log is missing or a balance unreliable, label the limitation rather than certify a complete account.

(b) Report suspected loss to an unconflicted authority with preserved sources, a bounded estimate and the affected period. A discrepancy is not proof of theft. The custodian may take an authorized narrow protective step, but may not seize a member's property or publish accusations as a substitute for Title II. A handover identifies open obligations and original unresolved operations so that a successor does not duplicate an uncertain transfer.

## Article III.2 — Ordinary dues, receipts and hardship

### Section III.2.1 — One obligation per verified member

(a) Ordinary dues remain one gold weekly with the fourteen-day new-member exemption. One verified member has one obligation in the designated home guild; linked alts do not multiply it. A receipt records the actual eligible transfer, period and authorized reviewer through the existing manual process. An officer's estimate, player-entered mail reference or a row prepared before transfer is a claim, not proof that gold arrived.

(b) Hardship and a disputed attribution receive private, respectful review without public balance lists. Record the necessary outcome and applicable period, not an intrusive story about a member's circumstances. Neither a missed entry nor an unpaid ledger automatically removes membership, changes a rank or proves misconduct. A material dispute is reconciled with the member and, where necessary, a second reviewer before any separate conduct process is considered.

### Section III.2.2 — Retention and correction of the ledger

(a) The established ninety-day ledger retention continues. Correct an inaccurate entry through an attributed adjustment referencing the original record; do not invent a fresh transfer or alter the original date to extend retention. A reversal, duplication or misattributed character is recorded with its effect on the eligible period. Access is limited to the actual duty, and member-facing reports do not reveal another person's private receipt or hardship status.

(b) Account export and serving-account erasure cover their registered attributable data under the published operating controls. A need for resource accountability is not a blanket licence to keep an erased ordinary profile or every audit entry. Necessary unresolved-effect, safety and recovery records have their own narrowly stated purposes and original clocks. A downloaded ledger or private recovery copy is not claimed physically erased merely because the live account cleanup completed.

## Article III.3 — Repair and consumable appropriations

### Section III.3.1 — A budget with a named public purpose

(a) A repair, raid-consumable, training or shared-project proposal states its purpose, eligible activity, expected resource need, funding source, ceiling, recipient or controlled distribution rule and settlement date. A budget is not permission for an unlimited withdrawal. Repairs and consumables should serve published participation criteria, not friendship, audience size, optional donations or secret priority. An unused allowance is not personal compensation.

(b) The responsible lead confirms the event's actual needs; the competent financial authority approves the appropriation; an authorized custodian executes it; and an independent reviewer reconciles it. Roles may be combined only where independence is not required, and combined roles are declared. No addon button or website budget row bypasses the game's protected actions or the operator's separately approved bank access.

### Section III.3.2 — Substantial transactions and settlement

(a) The existing substantial-transaction rule applies: an unbudgeted transfer exceeding ten gold, or any transfer exceeding ten percent of the last reconciled unrestricted balance, whichever threshold is reached first. Combine related transfers over seven days to prevent splitting. Without a reliable balance, treat every non-routine transfer as substantial. Two recorded approvals are required: the responsible Treasurer or Guild Master and an independent, unconflicted authorized approver; a recipient approves neither their own payment nor its audit.

(b) Retain the approval reference and actual receipt or available bank-log evidence, settle unused materials under the approved arrangement and explain material differences. A lead should provide settlement within seven calendar days of the event or publish a reasoned revised date. The weekly reconciliation and monthly independent review remain controlling cadences. A failed or uncertain external action retains its original identity; it is not retried as a new payment to make the ledger appear balanced.

## Article III.4 — The seasonal Cosmetic Tithe Tiers

### Section III.4.1 — Voluntary recognition and exact thresholds

(a) Cosmetic Tithe Roles recognise additional, voluntary in-game gold within each published ninety-day season: **Tithe Payer — 25 gold**, **Patron of the Treasury — 100 gold**, and **Benefactor of Olympus — 500 gold**. At most the highest currently qualified tier is displayed. Contributions are mailed to Fernmelder with a TITHE reference and verified against the actual receipt by the admitted Treasurer or an independently authorized reviewer. The reference alone is not payment proof.

(b) Ordinary weekly dues, a waived obligation and an unclassified historical receipt do not automatically become cosmetic credit. Publish the season's exact start and end before an award; no invented season is inferred from a deployment date. An award expires at that season's end and a new season requires its own eligible receipts. No lifetime donor balance, permanent rank, real-money payment, token purchase, outside-game consideration or automatic debit is part of this schedule.

### Section III.4.2 — Consent, zero privilege and implementation gates

(a) A member may decline public recognition without losing credit or standing. Publish only the consented tier, never the exact amount, receipt, private ledger, hardship status or payment correspondence. Recognition is cosmetic colour only: it gives no channels, native rank, Council seat or vote, bank right, raid or loot priority, admission preference, rule exemption or confidential access. Donations cannot purchase a hearing outcome or relief from discipline.

(b) Actual cosmetic Discord dispatch requires its separately qualified writer, configured zero-permission role below the bot, admitted operator and original-operation checks. All `TREASURY_COSMETIC_*` controls remain absent or OFF until separately authorized and configured. Publication of these thresholds creates no role or grant. Pending or uncertain awards remain labelled; a correction reconciles only the affected cosmetic recognition and never removes guild membership as punishment. Staff do not retain a private receipt beyond its proper policy merely to keep an expired colour visible.

## Article III.5 — Audit, disclosure and the opening of the vault

### Section III.5.1 — The audit trail and member account

(a) Each reviewed transaction links its purpose, approval, actual custody effect, reconciliation and correction, with dates sufficient to test the sequence. The independent auditor samples or examines the declared scope and identifies unavailable logs, incomplete periods and unresolved differences. A report names what was examined; it does not certify every transaction or a protected game operation never observed. The bank custodian supplies authorized read-only evidence without handing over credentials.

(b) Members receive understandable summaries of resources committed, material decisions and limitations without another person's payment details or sensitive case evidence. A monthly review can report whether reconciliation is complete and which service needs remain, rather than exposing a donor leaderboard. Any public figure carries its period and source status. A historical beta amount or stale observation is not a present spendable balance.

### Section III.5.2 — Objection, recovery and continuity

(a) A member may question an attribution or expenditure privately and receives the ordinary three-working-day acknowledgement and a named unconflicted reviewer. Preserve the original entry and evidence while assessing the correction. Serious alleged dishonesty follows Title II's standard and fair hearing; a treasury inquiry does not authorize public naming or automatic discipline. An appeal is available under the ordinary fourteen-day route after a consequential decision.

(b) Loss, vacancy or client incompatibility requires a documented continuity plan within existing authority. Recovery, replacement custody, privilege changes and disposal of copies each need their own real approval and verification. No claim of backup, equal counts or elapsed quiet time proves a safe production restore. The vault may remain held while authority or an original external effect is uncertain; honest incompleteness serves the guild better than a fabricated reconciliation.

# Title IV — The Grand Alliance Concordat

**Ten banners may share one fellowship without pretending to be one roster.** Olympus I, II, III, IV, V, VI, VII, VIII, IX and X are the named sister-branch directory places. Their presence in the Concordat does not prove ten active guilds, a live realm or appointed officers. The common banner coordinates PvE Alliance play while preserving optional PvP activity. Current launch realm remains unannounced; an old beta guild, queue or screenshot is dated evidence, never a fresh capacity report.

## Article IV.1 — Affiliation and the equality of branches

### Section IV.1.1 — Entry into the Concordat

(a) A branch affiliates through the competent authority's recorded instrument identifying its actual guild and realm, Guild Master, common obligations, local rules, accepted contacts and review arrangements. The Olympus I–X directory must distinguish a confirmed affiliation, vacant office, unappointed placeholder and unavailable report. Copying a branch name into an addon, approved-paste record or web directory does not establish the guild's existence or appoint its leadership.

(b) Each affiliated branch owes the same fair procedure and resource accountability. Numerical order denotes a banner, not a superior class of member. A person routed to a sister branch retains the protections of the common Charter. The Concordat does not merge private accounts, local bank assets or unrelated Discord authority, and a minister's coordination line does not turn every local Guild Master into that minister's subordinate.

### Section IV.1.2 — Common standards and local customs

(a) The joint Charters, common Statute and duly approved alliance measures govern shared matters. A branch may publish compatible local event schedules, mentoring arrangements and operational contacts. It cannot waive privacy protections, sell office or impose an undisclosed disciplinary code. A proposed local exception identifies the controlling provision and competent approver rather than declaring a conflict solved by local custom.

(b) The local Guild Master and emissary bring unresolved conflicts to the Chief of Staff for coordination, then the designated constitutional authority where necessary. Pending resolution, preserve the more protective applicable safeguard and avoid irreversible acts outside confirmed authority. A common rule's publication and a compatible technical client are separate dependencies. No branch is reported synchronized merely because a common file was exported.

## Article IV.2 — The thousand-seat limit and overflow routing

### Section IV.2.1 — Honest capacity evidence

(a) The planning ceiling is 1,000 guild characters per branch, subject to the actual game capability and the existing admitted capacity controls. A seat count states the branch, observed period, source, completeness, freshness and whether it is a community aggregate or an authoritative local roster. Neither an aggregate QR export nor the absence of a report proves a specific person's membership, an invite entitlement or an empty guild.

(b) Live capacity is unavailable when the required current trusted feed is unavailable; display unknown or unavailable rather than zero. The October Beta census of 1,000/1,000 and 247 queued belongs to the dated Beta Veterans / Founding Epoch archive. It is not added to current Olympus I–X totals or carried forward as a live queue. The census exporter remains aggregate counts only and cannot confer proof, rank or admission authority.

### Section IV.2.2 — A route onward without a promise of a seat

(a) When the current receiving branch is confirmed full, intake explains the available sister-branch option and obtains the member's choice. The receiving Guild Master or admitted intake officer confirms current eligibility and availability through the existing verification and attended invite process. A route suggestion is not a reservation, successful invite or guaranteed position. A stale or disputed count pauses the claim of availability without rejecting the person.

(b) Preserve the original application and necessary handoff reference; do not duplicate a person's queue entries or bypass a pending verification hold to make an overflow number look better. Tell the member which branch is proposed, which step remains and whom to contact. A transfer after admission requires the receiving branch's agreement and reconciliation of the home-guild record. No bot is authorized by this Title to move a character or execute a protected invite automatically.

## Article IV.3 — Main and Verified Alt standing

### Section IV.3.1 — One member, linked characters

(a) A member identifies a main and may link Verified Alt characters through the admitted identity process. A character label or census entry alone is not a verified relationship. Match the actual account, GUID and current guild evidence required by the implemented workflow; ambiguous names remain unresolved. A renaming requirement imposed by Blizzard follows the existing staff-confirmed fresh-application rule rather than an invented blanket punishment for every linked character.

(b) Main and Alt describe character relationships and operational placement, not unequal human worth. The recommended native Alt slot is applied only by the competent attended Guild Master. Officer Alt remains a historical extended label, not a new native slot. A second character supplies no second Council vote, duplicate ordinary dues obligation, automatic extra allocation or permission to bypass an account-level safeguard.

### Section IV.3.2 — Home branch, transfers and disputes

(a) Record the designated home guild for obligations counted per person and distinguish it from temporary event participation or an alt's branch. A main change or transfer identifies the effective period and avoids duplicate credit or a fictitious break in membership. Activity-specific raid or group rules may consider readiness and role without converting an alt classification into a general exclusion from the fellowship.

(b) A disputed link is handled privately through the actual verification or correction route. Officers must not demand account credentials, force a public disclosure of a private alt or infer ownership from a similar name. A missing or stale export makes the relevant evidence unknown; it does not establish desertion, deception or grounds for a purge. Data correction, membership review and disciplinary proceedings keep their separate authorities.

## Article IV.4 — Branch officers and cross-border jurisdiction

### Section IV.4.1 — Local command and shared portfolios

(a) The branch Guild Master appoints local officers within its accepted remit and remains responsible for actual local roster and bank controls. Emissaries for raiding, PvP, Finance, Artisanry and Heraldry coordinate information with the common portfolios. Their departmental correspondence is not command authority over another guild. A raid leader's tactical direction lasts for the assigned activity and cannot amend the Charter or borrow another branch's vault.

(b) A visiting officer acts by invitation and within a written local or common delegation. On an immediate risk, contact the competent local responder and preserve observations; an emergency action still requires actual permission, narrow scope and review. A cross-branch safety report is information for a fresh competent assessment, not a command to replicate a sanction. Local moderators do not become alliance-wide judges by forwarding a label.

### Section IV.4.2 — Shared cases and conflicting interests

(a) A case spanning branches names a lead unconflicted case owner and identifies which authority can enforce each possible remedy. Avoid parallel proceedings that conceal evidence, multiply punishments or prevent an appeal. Each affected person receives the same useful allegations and opportunity to respond. A common panel may make findings within its designation, while actual branch or platform actions remain separately authorized and recorded.

(b) Conflicts between branch officers go first to coordination where suitable, then to independent Justice review for disputed conduct or the constitutional authority for reserved jurisdiction. A branch's larger roster or an officer's public audience supplies no extra vote. The register records whose decision applies, its limited reach and the review route. An external or unaffiliated guild's rights are not presumed under an Olympus warrant.

## Article IV.5 — Reporting, continuity and withdrawal

### Section IV.5.1 — The alliance report

(a) Active branches review intake and urgent needs weekly; portfolio and emissary coordination is fortnightly and Council service review monthly under the existing cadence. Reports separate confirmed operations, voluntary plans and unresolved dependencies. Aggregate capacity is labelled by source and age; personal records stay in their authorized system. A delayed report is an administrative matter to resolve, not an attendance rule for ordinary members.

(b) The approved I–X and Council authority export, aggregate census and verification records are distinct products. Publisher incorporation, a compatible official addon and actual in-game testing remain necessary where the workflow depends on them. No aggregate count, hashed identifier or successful source test substitutes for a current protected game action. Keys and activation profiles remain held until their separate approvals; this Concordat changes no launch gate.

### Section IV.5.2 — End of affiliation and the honourable handover

(a) A proposed withdrawal identifies the competent authority, affected common obligations, notice to members, custody handover and treatment of open cases. Give a practical opportunity to resolve outstanding matters without holding a person's account or private property hostage. Ending affiliation does not authorize destruction of records, erasure of a complaint, removal of unrelated server safety roles or an unapproved bulk change to member permissions.

(b) The directory records the effective change and leaves a clear historical trace without pretending the old branch is still live. Members receive honest options for continuing fellowship and fresh receiving-branch checks. Sensitive records follow their actual privacy and retention purpose. What remains uncertain is carried into the handover with its original provenance; no departing custodian or successor may recast an unknown provider effect as a confirmed success.

# Title V — The Guild Workshop & Artisans' Charter and The Heralds' Accord

**Let the forge make service visible, and the herald make truth audible.** `Crafters.lua` and `Workshop.lua` name the craft and commission divisions; `Vox.lua` and `Hop.lua` name the communication and layer-coordination divisions. These names describe cooperation across the wider addon ecosystem, not a claim that the upstream publisher has shipped every facility. Voluntary directory participation, actual current client capability, protected game actions and separately qualified website features remain distinct.

## Article V.1 — The opt-in Workshop and the artisan's standing

### Section V.1.1 — A freely offered craft

(a) The Workshop is an opt-in directory of Forever professions, recipes and willing help. A listing identifies the chosen profession, offered recipe or service, optional skill claim, availability and last update, with self-declared information visibly distinguished from independent confirmation. A member chooses what to publish and may withdraw it through the admitted controls. Being a guild member, owning a rare recipe or accepting an office does not consent to an automatic public listing.

(b) The directory is a way to find a conversation, not proof of character ownership, current stock, completed work or a guaranteed response. Use only the implemented bounded fields and registered account-data controls. Private trades, exact real-world schedules, account credentials and another person's identity or location do not belong in an offer. The actual publication audience is stated; a signed-in guild directory is not described as an unrestricted public advertisement.

### Section V.1.2 — Stewardship without monopoly

(a) The Minister of Artisanry maintains useful categories, answers correction requests and helps willing crafters coordinate. No ministry may compel a player to reveal a private recipe collection, work for free or surrender an item. Artisan, Crafter, Profession Coordinator and Quartermaster are service remits; they grant no withdrawal, officer-chat, disciplinary or private-ledger access merely by their name.

(b) Where a feature is disabled, unavailable or awaiting qualification, the Workshop says so and directs members to an approved human contact. It must not show fabricated offers or represent an unanswered opt-in as consent. A stale listing is labelled or withdrawn through the authorized process, never cited as a broken promise. A member's erasure or withdrawal follows the actual original-operation safeguards rather than an improvised deletion of unrelated records.

## Article V.2 — Commission routing and the artisan's ledger

### Section V.2.1 — The commission before the hammer falls

(a) A request states the desired item or service, game-version context, materials supplied or still needed, optional consideration, intended recipient, reasonable handoff window and the member's permitted contact route. The receiving artisan confirms what they can undertake before a request becomes an accepted commission. A directory click, message delivery or ministry assignment alone does not establish agreement or a completed trade.

(b) Route requests by actual recipe availability, willing capacity and the requester's choice. Do not sell raid priority, office or verification access through a crafting queue. Minimise personal data in a shared intake list and keep sensitive trade details between the competent parties. The guild does not promise real-money settlement, hold account credentials or guarantee a player's stock. No system should imply escrow where none has been separately established.

### Section V.2.2 — Changes, completion and a disputed handoff

(a) Agree material substitutions, changed quantities and revised timing before work where practicable. A completion claim identifies the accepted scope and actual handoff evidence; an item crafted, an item mailed and an item received are distinct facts. If a party is unavailable, record that status without publishing allegations. A coordinator should acknowledge a raised service dispute within three working days and propose a realistic next contact date.

(b) Where common-bank materials are requested, Title III's appropriation, independent approval and reconciliation rules apply before custody changes. A crafter cannot approve their own payment or convert a ministry note into withdrawal permission. Ordinary disagreement may use voluntary mediation; a serious fraud allegation follows Title II with evidence, response and appeal. No blacklist or cross-branch sanction is inferred from a late reply or an unconfirmed delivery.

## Article V.3 — Rare Forever recipes and the common store of knowledge

### Section V.3.1 — Provenance, discovery and fair access

(a) A rare Forever recipe is recorded with its actual game version, source of knowledge and verification status. A screenshot, self-report and observed craft are different evidence. Do not publish a rumour as a confirmed drop location or carry a beta recipe into launch as guaranteed. The artisan may offer teaching or production voluntarily; the guild honours the discovery without declaring personal ownership of a player's account, recipe book or time.

(b) A shared recipe or material funded by the guild has an appropriation recording its purpose, intended steward, allowed use and handover arrangement before expenditure. Selection uses published service criteria and conflict disclosure. It does not promise an impossible transfer of a bound recipe, compel prohibited sharing or override the actual client. An unavailable game capability stays unavailable, even when a ceremony calls the item a treasure of the realm.

### Section V.3.2 — Library, continuity and attribution

(a) Shared guides name their contributor with consent, source, version and last check. Distinguish original advice, attributed external material and experiment. The Ministry corrects a material error visibly and preserves the relevant edition's history without storing unnecessary private identifiers. A recipe reference is useful evidence of knowledge, never a membership credential or verification proof.

(b) A departing steward offers a documented handover of the guild-funded materials actually in their custody and the approved reference notes. No demand extends to passwords, private account assets or prohibited game actions. A new custodian receives only separately approved access. If a promised craft cannot be completed after a version change, explain the limitation and seek an agreed remedy instead of inventing a successful transaction.

## Article V.4 — The Heralds' Accord and responsible publication

### Section V.4.1 — The voice of Olympus

(a) `Vox.lua` is the named communication division. A herald or correspondent distinguishes an adopted decision, an approved announcement, a proposed event and personal reporting. Verify source, destination, effective time and time zone; avoid claims that an unannounced launch realm is settled. Correct material errors with a dated reference. A herald may speak with warmth and ceremony while keeping facts, quotations and inference visibly separate.

(b) Officer digests, private casework, exact contribution records, unpublished drafts and protected account information do not enter public news. Use only the approved audience and current publication permission. A saved draft, preview or click is not proof of an external post. Automatic mentions are disabled by default, and a lost sending answer retains its original operation for a permitted check rather than a blind second proclamation.

### Section V.4.2 — Names, audience and the right of correction

(a) Publication of a member's name or likeness follows the current directory, consent and appointment rules, with extra details and Workshop offers remaining voluntary. Staff must not treat every person in Asmongold's Discord server as an Olympus directory entry. A source already visible elsewhere does not make every republishing purpose appropriate. Private help and account-data controls remain available without forcing a member to expose a concern on stream.

(b) A correction request is acknowledged through the ordinary service cadence and routed to a person who can amend the relevant publication. If an external copy cannot be edited or its removal is unconfirmed, say so. A herald's correction does not prove that every screenshot or device copy disappeared. Retain only the evidence needed for the original operation and applicable privacy purpose; a ceremonial archive is no general exception to erasure or retention limits.

## Article V.5 — Stream Shield and the broadcaster's peace

### Section V.5.1 — Presentation protection with honest limits

(a) Stream Shield is a local presentation preference for a member preparing to broadcast. Its control must have an intelligible accessible name, clear active state and a usable compact layout. Turning it ON should help a broadcaster avoid accidental display of the covered site details; it does not change server authorization, substitute for a deliberate scene check or guarantee that a private field can never appear in another page, browser tab, notification or recording.

(b) A Broadcaster, Content Creator, VIP or Content Rep has no exemption from ordinary access rules. Do not ask a performer to disclose private records for entertainment or treat an unreadable screen as reviewed. The broadcaster checks the actual visible scene before going live and respects the guild's publication boundaries. Shield state is never evidence of membership, consent to public naming, a safe protected action or deletion of the underlying data.

### Section V.5.2 — Harassment, evidence and response

(a) Staff respond to concrete, readable abuse through the competent moderation route, preserving a bounded original observation and any coverage gaps. A name on a stream, allegation in chat or ordinary system entry does not identify an offender or prove intent. Reports describe what was actually observed and avoid exposing invitation codes, private messages, account credentials or unrelated participants. No officer may retaliate against a person for using Stream Shield or declining publicity.

(b) An immediate credible risk may justify Title I's narrow temporary measure and independent review; a lasting sanction follows Title II. Platform reporting remains available to the affected person. Guild staff do not promise surveillance, unseen-screen coverage or professional safety services. A practical incident handoff identifies the evidence, authority and next review time, leaving uncertain identity and unavailable facts explicitly unresolved.

## Article V.6 — Layer coordination and the closing of the workshop bell

### Section V.6.1 — Invitations to gather, not commands to automate

(a) `Hop.lua` is the named layer-coordination division. A willing group may share an event rendezvous and coordinate through the actual game's permitted group and layer behaviour. State the event, consenting group lead and current observed conditions without promising a fixed layer, bypass of a cap or successful transfer. A received invitation, accepted group and completed arrival are different observations.

(b) No herald, addon or directory gains authority to automate protected invites, spam players, evade platform limits or require account sharing. Members may decline a move or leave a group without a disciplinary presumption. Do not publish another person's precise location or availability without consent. Where the client cannot verify a layer or the relevant feature is absent, provide ordinary human coordination and label the technical limit.

### Section V.6.2 — Service review and the unclosed gates

(a) Artisanry and Heraldry review unresolved requests, stale offers and publication corrections during their existing fortnightly coordination, with material risks taken to the monthly Council review. These are service cadences for accepted officers, not compulsory play hours. Reports say what was delivered, its evidence and limits, who holds the next action and when it will be reviewed. An empty directory or a disabled feed is reported honestly, not filled with invented activity.

(b) Publisher adoption of the official addon, real-game validation, Watcher installation, key custody, protected bank and invite actions, recovery authority, Olympus Link activation, aggregate Army publication and cosmetic-role dispatch retain their separate gates. This code provides no new schema, grant, deployment, recovery, cryptographic trust root or permission by implication. Until the real competent actions are completed, their instruments remain unexecuted, their controls remain OFF or unavailable, and the fellowship may still gather freely beneath the banner.
