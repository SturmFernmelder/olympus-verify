# Old beta removal entry fence — source preparation, 16 October 2026

This follow-on to the dispatch-fence component covers the three receipt-less DELETE sites in `roles.ts`: `revokeHeld`, `revokeForBan`, and `removeIfBlocked`. It is stacked on `7e8dea41` (tree `cd68ba59`) as `76e7e3c4` (tree `a6d520e1`); it was originally authored on `149cb007`. It changes neither the reset mandate nor the provider's outcome, and issues no seal or receipt.

The physical scope is exactly `BETA_RESET_GUILD_ID` and the twelve source-defined `BETA_RESET_ROLE_IDS`. The new mode witness is captured synchronously at the entry of each `roles.ts` export, before that export's first await; callers in interactions, rename-review, restore, backfill and roster were not threaded by this patch (see the 17 October cut below). It reads only the mode's descriptor and makes no policy/database request. A copied witness or another environment cannot authorize the old path. Accessor and inherited mode properties are refused without invoking a getter.

For these targets, exact `v1`, every other present mode, or a changed original descriptor holds the old removal. Only unchanged absence retains its original path. Until the central removal consumers are converted, v1 also refuses delegation to `settleMembershipRemoval`; it does not silently substitute a presently unfenced durable path. Unrelated role IDs and guilds keep their original behavior.

`revokeForReapply`, `grantMemberRole`, and `reconcileBanned` retain their original capture for private `revokeHeld` and the ban callback. They do not recapture after the member read, D1 cause check, or an acknowledged grant. The dynamic-import branch checks that same witness immediately before central delegation. Existing provider calls, SQL, audit/ACK/failure reporting and mandatory-removal accounting remain unchanged when the mode is absent. A mode change after a DELETE was already dispatched does not suppress its original success/failure audit.

## Deliberate remaining boundary

An initially absent-mode call may still enter the unchanged central consumer. Mode changes during that consumer's later awaits remain the responsibility of the separately owned `role-settlements.ts` integration. The direct removals in `roster.ts` and `interactions.ts`, central creation/claim/pre-effect predicates, compensation producers, and previously accepted provider work are not closed by this patch (the 17 October cut below closes the first three, with the residuals listed there). It is not an all-writer barrier, a completed drain, or permission to reuse a role. Existing spent/unknown rows are not altered.

The new `worker/tests/beta_old_removal_fence_test.cjs` uses the real production modules and existing installed-epoch Native fixture in both layouts. It covers all twelve IDs; v1 and invalid descriptors; unchanged absent and outside-scope effects; mode drift during GET, D1 read and dynamic import; sweep and post-grant callback capture; untouched acknowledged grant custody; and post-dispatch ACK/failure reporting. Existing test files are unchanged. Run when the shared runtime is available: `node tests/beta_old_removal_fence_test.cjs` from `worker/`, followed by the relevant existing role/removal regressions and TypeScript. No deployment or activation is authorized. Rolling back this source restores the old removal paths; removing a mode is not evidence that earlier invocations or provider effects ended.

**Rule.** The scope is `BETA_RESET_GUILD_ID` and the twelve `BETA_RESET_ROLE_IDS`. New removal dispatch on one of these fixed targets covers creating a removal intent, a spent removal row or a retirement or compensation child, claiming one, and sending its DELETE. It is allowed only when three things hold: the mode captured at the originating entry was absent, its descriptor is unchanged at the check, and the env the effect runs under carries no `BETA_RESET_DISPATCH_SEAL_MODE` descriptor of its own, whether own data, accessor or inherited. Exact `v1`, every other present value (accessor and inherited included; no getter is ever called) and any change hold new dispatch.

At the central writers in `role-settlements.ts`, some work stays available in every mode: GET-only reconciliation of spent rows, and the ACK-free receipts that need no DELETE. These are `fresh_server_confirmed`, `absent`, `past_effect_absent`, the beta GET-only observations, the attended child recovery and the pair's no-write `absent` result. This holds at the central writers only. Entries that still hold v1 before reaching a central writer do not reach this work while v1 is set; they are listed in the "Export and handler holds" bullet under Remaining boundary.

Fresh targets and other guilds are not restricted, with one exception. A fixed current membership target holds its whole delegation on the new-dispatch path, so a fresh retired partner in the same pair is not removed either (`role-settlements.ts:609`). A fixed retired target paired with a fresh current target holds only the retired removal.

The check itself is pure synchronous JavaScript: descriptor reads, one `in` test, a WeakMap lookup and a descriptor comparison. It runs no SQL, await, policy read, seal read or receipt write. The central hold branches write nothing. On the two raw paths the hold branch writes one `role.remove_held {reason:'beta_dispatch_mode_held'}` audit, and only in non-absent mode (`interactions.ts:418`, `roster.ts:1003`). With the mode absent and unchanged, no SQL text, bind, provider request or their order changes; the receipt below checks this. Shared ACK and unknown writers are untouched.

**Capture threading.** Each capture is taken synchronously before its function's first await and is never retaken after one. It travels as `RemovalDispatch = {env, capture}` (`role-settlements.ts:33`), so it is checked against the env it was read from, and a spread env cannot replace it. Cross-env (finding 6): another env can add a hold but never lift one. `removalHeld` (`role-settlements.ts:43`) and `departureLegacy` (`roster.ts:977`) also hold a fixed target when the effect env itself carries any mode descriptor. `roles.settleGuildDepartureRole` has no check of its own any more (review fix 1, `item15-fixes-run.json`). It passes the entry pair straight to `settleMembershipRemoval` (`roles.ts:391`), whose `removalHeld` applies the same cross-env restriction. This addition only ever holds, and it is false when the mode is absent.
