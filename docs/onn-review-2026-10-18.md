## Priorities for the Olympus redesign

1. **Navigation and next action:** group existing pages into Guild, Community, Resources and Account; expose Staff only with its actual authority. Add a searchable page/action index, breadcrumbs and a stable “what do I do next?” area.
2. **Census and readiness:** visible last-successful-refresh time, source, stale/partial indicators, per-guild drill-down and timezone-aware events. A green flag must not imply verified membership, role grant or launch eligibility.
3. **Help at the point of use:** separate member/officer guides; short setup steps and safe download provenance; links from failed/held states to the exact recovery explanation.
4. **News/governance clarity:** pinned official notices, dates, category filters, historical archive and visibly distinct adopted/current versus historical/summary material.
5. **Keep the guild site focused:** preserve fast task access. Avoid compulsory live streams/chat, general-news aggregation, public naming/shaming, another world database, or a new unsigned desktop client.

This review recommends interaction and information-architecture patterns, not copying ONN's artwork, prose, software, data or branding. No Olympus feature flag, launch gate, staff mapping, erasure/reset setting or provider configuration was changed.
