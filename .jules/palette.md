## 2026-10-30 — Shared resource status boundary

Addons, Guides and automatic Updates share one message-node implementation. Scope explicit polite, atomic result-count announcements to the two catalogues: Updates already uses `role="status"` for its existing fetch/Pause feedback. Preserve that behavior rather than adding catalogue announcements to polling or removing its status role. Renderer tests can verify semantics and focused-node identity; they do not prove native screen-reader speech.
