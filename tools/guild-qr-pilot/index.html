What this pilot can and cannot establish

It accepts one own-guild aggregate observation from the separate pilot addon. Character names are checked transiently inside that addon for roster completeness, then discarded. No player names, ranks, notes, chat, SavedVariables or account details are exported. A volunteer can still fabricate the counts. It cannot grant a role, confirm a member, link an account or change an Olympus setting.

Capture ends on Stop, a hidden page, source interruption, decoding error or the fixed deadline. A complete preview expires after at most 15 minutes, with no refresh-on-read. No report is retained after clearing or reloading.

Desktop and mobile browser layouts are part of this pilot; successful real game, camera and client compatibility testing must be recorded separately.
